1. THE IDEA IN ONE BREATH
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Akayza gives spaza shops and township builders a record of their
  business that the people they trade with have confirmed, and that
  they own. They run the business on it every day: a credit book,
  "order again" from the suppliers they already use, and cash handed
  over that BOTH sides confirm on their phones, even without signal.
  Every payment and every signed-off building stage is sealed into a
  tamper-evident record the owner can share by link and take back.

  ONE LINE FOR THE PITCH:
    "Your business, proven by the people you trade with."

  WHAT IT IS NOT: not a marketplace, not a credit score, not a lender.
  Cash confirmed by both sides counts the same as digital payment.

4. THE DEMO
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  50-SECOND DEMO (sales room)
    0-10s   Nomsa records a R40 credit sale; it lands in her credit
            book.
    10-25s  Her runner Sbu collects stock with a one-time pass. Cash
            R2,340: both phones confirm -> green "Confirmed by both".
            Her record crosses into "Trusted" (seeded at 599 -> 600).
    25-35s  She shares her record; the link opens on a phone: reasons,
            "Record intact, verified just now".
    35-45s  Tamper: R2,340 changed to R23,400 in the database. Refresh
            -> "Record frozen". (tamper_demo.py, --restore after)
    45-50s  Builder: a stage signed off by the homeowner, sealed.

  5-MINUTE FINALE: same flow, slower, plus the dispute screen, the
  builder's portfolio link, revoke, and the ML-DSA check.

  Seed rules (spec section 8): every seeded event goes through the real
  sealing code; seed_demo.py asserts chains verify and Nomsa sits at
  599 before every rehearsal. All demo data is labelled synthetic.


5. HOW WE MAKE MONEY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  TRADERS AND BUILDERS -- FREE AT LAUNCH, PRO LATER
    Free   credit book, ordering, cash confirmation, jobs, My record.
           Always. No tool is ever locked by the reputation.
    Pro    ~R49 / month (est.), later: reports, stock count, staff and
           multiple phones, VAS tools, priority support.

  SUPPLIERS
    Starter (independent supplier)       R299 / month (est.)
    Pro (wholesaler, drivers)            R1,499 / month (est.)
    ERP integration (later)              R4,999 / month + R15,000 setup
                                         (est.)

  COMMISSION: ~1% of each PayFast order (est.), through PayFast split
    payments, so we never hold anyone's money. PayFast's own fee (card
    3.2% + R2, Instant EFT 2.0%, ex VAT) is separate. None on cash.

  VERIFICATION (NEW -- the revenue that matches the problem)
    Funders, programmes and licensed lenders pay to check a record the
    trader CHOSE to share with them: ~R50 per verified record check
    (est.), or a programme licence for fund administrators. The trader
    never pays for this and must consent every time. We never lend and
    never sell data.

  COSTS (researched 24 Sep 2026, $1 = R16.50; details in 07 section 3)
    Pilot stage: ~R460 / month (DigitalOcean app + managed PostgreSQL;
      Cloudflare, Cloudinary, email, Mapbox, Sentry on free tiers).
    Growth stage: ~R4,000-R4,700 / month (AWS Cape Town for data in SA,
      Cloudflare Pro, Cloudinary Plus, Resend Pro, Sentry).
    No WhatsApp/SMS costs: sign-in is email + Google.
    Break-even: pilot 2 suppliers on Starter; growth ~4 on Pro.

  THE ASK: R300,000 for a 6-month pilot (est.)
      2 builder stipends (R8,000 x 2 x 6)        R96,000
      1 field agent (R10,000 x 6)                R60,000
      running costs (R4,000 x 6)                 R24,000
      pilot data bundles (100 traders, R100/mo)  R60,000

                                          total R300,000
    Pilot sites: Polokwane / Seshego and Tembisa. One wholesaler and
