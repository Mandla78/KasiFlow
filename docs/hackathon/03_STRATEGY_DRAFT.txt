2. PROBLEM STATEMENT (mentor session Fri 19:00; max 2 paragraphs)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  South Africa's township economy is worth about R900 billion a year,
  and spaza shops alone about R180 billion. Yet nearly four in five
  township businesses are unregistered and fewer than 9% can get a
  bank loan (Standard Bank Township Informal Economy Report, 2025).
  The trade runs on cash, WhatsApp and a notebook: a spaza reorders
  from the same wholesaler every week by phone, pays the driver in
  cash, and neither side gets a record both of them agree on.

  Without a record, a real business can't prove it exists. When
  government opened the R500 million Spaza Shop Support Fund, 354
  applications failed verification after site visits, and only
  R179.6 million had been allocated by May 2026 (DSBD, 29 May 2026).
  [ONE LINE OF LIVED EXPERIENCE HERE: a spaza owner or supplier from
  our validation calls, first name + permission.]


3. THE OFFER, IN ONE BREATH
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Akayza is the free business app for spaza shops (and builders):
  a credit book, one-tap reorders from the suppliers they ALREADY buy
  from, paid by PayFast or confirmed-by-both cash, and a record the
  owner owns and can share to prove the business is real.

  The insight: informal businesses already know who they buy from.
  We don't build a marketplace or recommend products; we make the
  existing supplier relationship digital, paid and provable.

  supplier's money. Cash orders: no commission.
  PHASE 2 (large trader base): trader premium subscription (~R49/month

9. THE QUANTUM BONUS (5 points we can actually earn)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE REAL REASON: a trader's record must stay trustworthy for years.
  A future quantum computer could forge today's Ed25519 signatures,
  so an old record could be faked. SHA-256 hashing stays safe.

  OUTLINE (2 points): "Records are post-quantum ready: hybrid
    signatures, Ed25519 + ML-DSA (NIST FIPS 204)."
  IMPLEMENT (the rest): the SERVER's seal on each record event is
    signed twice, Ed25519 + ML-DSA-65. The public record page shows
    "post-quantum signature verified". Library: @noble/post-quantum
    (MIT, supports ML-DSA per FIPS 204; pure JS, not constant-time,
    which is fine for verifying public records). Phones keep Ed25519
    this weekend; roadmap: phone-side hybrid.
  COST: about 2-3 hours. Do it after the must-haves, never before.
