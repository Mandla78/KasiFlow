ROUND 1 -- 3-MINUTE SALES PITCH (Sun 10:00-12:30)
"Pay full prize, pay half, or not buying."
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  0:00  PROBLEM (20 s)
        "Bongani builds rooms in Seshego. Nomsa runs a spaza in
        Tembisa. Both run real businesses on cash, WhatsApp and a
        notebook, so neither has a history a funder can trust. 38% of
        construction workers
        are informal. When government opened the Spaza Shop Support
        Fund, 354 applications failed verification."
  0:20  OFFER (30 s)
        "Akayza is a free app that helps informal businesses run
        better today and get funded tomorrow. Builders run jobs in
        stages that the client signs off. Spaza shops use a credit book
        and order stock from their suppliers, and see suppliers near
        them who sell what they buy. Payments made in the app are
        confirmed by the payment provider, and records can't be quietly
        changed, so the history holds up."
  0:50  DEMO (50 s)
        sign-up (4 steps) -> suppliers near you -> builder stage
        sign-off -> order + digital payment confirmed -> tamper check
        trips
  1:40  WHY US (20 s)
        "Not a marketplace: we connect traders with the suppliers they
        already trust, and suppliers get orders straight into their own
        systems. Security is built in: verified sign-in, one trusted
        phone per account, verified payments, tamper-evident records."
  2:00  MONEY + ASK (40 s)
        "Traders use it free. Suppliers pay R299 to R1,499 a month, plus
        about 1% on digital orders; funders pay to verify a record the
        trader chose to share. Running costs are about R460 a month in
        the pilot. We need R300,000 for a 6-month pilot in Polokwane and
        Tembisa."
  2:40  CLOSE (20 s)
        "Akayza: keep it in the kasi. Run your business today, get
        funded tomorrow. Are you buying?"
