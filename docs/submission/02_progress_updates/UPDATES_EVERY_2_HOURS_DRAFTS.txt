#3 -- SAT 02:00   account security + privacy + photos       BUILT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCORE: 3

ACCOMPLISHED
  - New phone, second check: signing in on an unknown phone needs an
    email code; the phone is then trusted for 90 days.
  - Sessions: see where you're signed in and sign out other phones.
  - Privacy: download my data (one tap), close my account, consent
    recorded at sign-up, and unverified accounts removed automatically.
  - Audit trail: every security event (sign-ins, failed attempts, resets,
    profile changes) is written to an append-only log, never containing
    passwords, codes or links.
  - Profile photos: uploaded straight from the phone to secure storage
    with a one-time signed upload; the server checks the real size,
    ownership and a daily limit, and verifies the storage service's
    signed notifications.

CHALLENGES
  Photo uploads on Android: the image picker's crop screen crashed on
  some phones, and a stray tap could remove a photo. We removed the crop
  step and added a confirmation before anything is deleted.

SCORE: 4

ACCOMPLISHED
  The credit book is complete (app + backend, tested):
  - Record a credit sale in a few taps: the customer (search or add, with
    an optional cellphone), the amount, the date given and the date due,
    like a paper book.
  - Record part payments; see who owes what and what is overdue.

  book). It's logged with a test that will pass once it's fixed.

#5 -- SAT 06:00   jobs for builders                          BUILT (Risuna)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCORE: 4

ACCOMPLISHED
  Jobs for builders are complete (app + backend, tested):
  - A job is split into stages whose amounts must add up to the total.
  - Each stage gets a photo taken with the camera (never the gallery).
    The server stamps its own time and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the
    stored photo, so a swapped or back-dated photo is detectable.
  - Client sign-off without an app: the builder sends a link; the client
    types the cash they paid. Matching amounts are confirmed by both;
    different amounts are kept as "amounts don't match".
  - The sign-off link is a one-use ticket that expires in 7 days, stored
    only as a keyed hash; a newer link cancels the old one.

CHALLENGES
  WhatsApp and phone browsers: links to a bare address aren't clickable
  and phones force https, so sign-off links now go through a secure
  https address.

#8 -- SAT 12:00   cart + checkout                            BUILT (test data)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCORE: 6

ACCOMPLISHED
  - A cart per supplier, with the supplier's minimum order checked and
    the total always in view.
  - Checkout in three steps: delivery (to the business or another
    address) or collection, how to pay, then a review.
  - Payment rules: digital payment is recommended; cash is offered only
    if the supplier takes it, up to R1,000 per order, with at most two
    cash orders waiting at a time. Cash orders can be cancelled until the
    supplier accepts; paid digital orders can't.
  - The server prices every order again from the catalogue; the phone
    never decides the price.

CHALLENGES
  Balancing cash and safety: many traders still pay cash, but a cash
  order costs nothing up front, so fake orders are cheap. Limits per
  order and per trader keep that risk small.
