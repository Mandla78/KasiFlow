#1 -- FRIDAY 23:59  (identity only; everything below is built and tested)

  WHERE IS YOUR TEAM?   3

  WHAT HAS BEEN ACCOMPLISHED?
  Our secure account foundation is complete, end to end (mobile app +
  backend), with 271 automated tests:
  - Sign-up with email and password, confirmed by a 6-digit email code
    (expires in 15 minutes, 5 tries), sent through real email templates;
    codes are never written to logs.
  - Sign-in with short-lived access tokens (15 min) and refresh tokens
    that rotate on every use; sessions can be viewed and signed out
    remotely ("sign out other phones").
  - Two-step check on a new phone: an email code, then the phone is
    trusted for 90 days.
  - Forgot password: a one-use reset link (30 minutes) that works from
    the email on any device.
  - Protection: rate limits on every account route, lockout after 5
    failed sign-ins (15 min), strict input checks (hidden characters,
    fake names, oversized values refused), passwords hashed, secrets
    only in environment settings.
  - Privacy: download my data, close my account, consent recorded at
    sign-up, unverified accounts cleaned up automatically, and an
    append-only audit trail of every security event.

  WHAT WILL YOU FOCUS ON NEXT?
  The business tools traders use every day, built on this foundation,
  then connecting traders to their suppliers.

  WHAT CHALLENGES ARE YOU FACING?
  - Links in messages: WhatsApp doesn't open links that point at a local
    development address, so we moved all links to a secure https address.
  - Running the full test suite takes about 12 minutes on our laptops;
