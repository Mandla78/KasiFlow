#1 -- FRIDAY 22:00  (auth only)

  WHERE IS YOUR TEAM?   2

  WHAT HAS BEEN ACCOMPLISHED?
  Secure sign-up and sign-in are complete, end to end (mobile app +
  backend), with automated tests:
  - Sign-up with email and password, confirmed by a 6-digit email code
    (expires in 15 minutes, 5 tries), sent through real email templates.
    Codes and links are never written to logs.
  - Sign-in with short-lived access tokens (15 minutes) and refresh tokens
    that rotate on every use.
  - Forgot password: a one-use reset link (a ticket that expires in 30
    minutes), stored only as a hash.
  - Protection: rate limits on every sign-up and sign-in route, lockout
    after 5 failed sign-ins (15 minutes), passwords hashed, and strict
    input checks (hidden characters, fake names and oversized values are
    refused).

  WHAT WILL YOU FOCUS ON NEXT?
  The sign-up steps after the account is created: a short onboarding that
  asks only for what we can verify and actually use, so we can personalise
  each user's app and power our recommendations from day one.

  WHAT CHALLENGES ARE YOU FACING?
  Setting up a secure https tunnel (ngrok) so we can test the full
  password reset on real phones: the one-use reset ticket expires after 30
  minutes, and links to a local development address don't open from email
  or WhatsApp on a phone. We now send every link through the tunnel's
  https address and are testing it end to end.
