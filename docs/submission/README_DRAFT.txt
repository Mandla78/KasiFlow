PRESENTATION FORMATS (from the organisers)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ROUND 1, SALES PITCH: 3 minutes, "just sell". The panel decides:
    pay full prize, pay half, or not buying. Their pro tip:
      problem with evidence (~20 s) -> what we offer and how it works
      -> HOW MUCH IT COSTS and HOW MUCH WE NEED NOW -> why we stand out
      -> quick demo (~50 s)
  TOP 12 FINALE: 5 minutes (mostly demo) + 3 minutes judges' Q&A.
  DEMO VIDEO: under 90 seconds; link on the Sonke project overview AND
    in the slides.
  SLIDES: more than 10. Must include: problem statement justification
    (charts, pictures), solution offering, user journey story,
    technological architecture, competitive analysis, data privacy
    policies, TRL, go-to-market, team, security, impact.
