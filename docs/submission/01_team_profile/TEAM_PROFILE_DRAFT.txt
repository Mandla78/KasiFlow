--- BACKGROUND: What inspired / motivated this project? ------------------

South Africa's informal economy runs on trust that leaves no trace.
Construction employs about 1.2 million people, 38% of them informally
(ILO, 2024), and small and micro contractors now carry more than half
of the sector's jobs (Business Day, July 2026). Government warns
homeowners about builders who disappear after payment and tells them
to keep payment records (Vuk'uzenzele, June 2026). Spaza shops move
about R180 billion a year inside a R900 billion township economy, yet
nearly 4 in 5 township businesses are unregistered and fewer than 9%
can get a bank loan (Standard Bank, 2025).

Payments are cash, agreements are verbal, and records live in
notebooks and WhatsApp, so real businesses cannot prove they are real.
When government opened the R500 million Spaza Shop Support Fund, 354
applications failed verification and only R179.6 million had been
allocated by May 2026 (DSBD). We are building the tool that turns the
way informal businesses already work into proof.


--- OVERVIEW: What is the project about? ---------------------------------

Akayza is an app for informal traders: builders, spaza shops and
every hustle. It turns the way they already work into proof.

FOR TRADERS (our users, free): business tools they use every day.
Builders run jobs in stages, with photo evidence and a sign-off from
the client. Spaza shops keep a credit book and reorder from the
suppliers they already buy from. Every payment, by PayFast or in cash
that both sides confirm on their phones (even without signal), is
sealed into a tamper-evident record the trader owns, can share by link
with a client, supplier or funder, and can take back.

FOR SUPPLIERS (our paying customers): wholesalers, regional
distributors and hardware stores receive orders and confirmed payments
from the traders they already serve, straight into their own systems
through our integration API.

Hybrid by design: traders use Akayza free to run their business;
suppliers pay because their own customers bring them in.


--- TECHNOLOGIES: List all the technologies used --------------------------

- Mobile: React Native + Expo (TypeScript), offline-first (SQLite),
  keys in the Android Keystore
- Backend: Python + Flask, domain-driven design, PostgreSQL, REST API
- Sign-in: email verification + Google Sign-In, JWT, PIN / fingerprint
- Supplier integration API: API keys, signed webhooks (ERP-ready)
- Payments: PayFast (sandbox), server-verified payment notifications
- Media: Cloudinary, with malware scanning on every upload
- Maps: Mapbox
- Security: Cloudflare (WAF, DDoS protection, TLS); Ed25519 signatures
  and SHA-256 hash chains for tamper-evident records; ML-DSA
  post-quantum signatures (NIST FIPS 204) on sealed records
