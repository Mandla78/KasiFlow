--- BACKGROUND: What inspired / motivated this project? ------------------

South Africa's informal economy runs on trust that leaves no trace.
Construction employs about 1.2 million people, 38% of them informally
(ILO, 2024), and small and micro contractors now carry more than half
of the sector's jobs (Business Day, July 2026). Government warns
homeowners about builders who disappear after payment and tells them
to keep payment records (Vuk'uzenzele, June 2026). Spaza shops move
about R180 billion a year inside a R900 billion township economy, yet
nearly 4 in 5 township businesses are unregistered and fewer than 9%
can get a bank loan (Standard Bank, 2025).

Payments are cash, agreements are verbal, and records live in
notebooks and WhatsApp, so real businesses cannot show a history that
funders can trust. When government opened the R500 million Spaza Shop
Support Fund, 354 applications failed verification and only R179.6
million had been allocated by May 2026 (DSBD). We are building an app
that helps informal businesses run better every day, and over time
builds the verified business history they need to access financing.


--- OVERVIEW: What is the project about? ---------------------------------

Akayza is an app for informal businesses: builders and trades, spaza
shops and other small businesses. It helps them run their business day
to day and prepares them for financing in the future.

FOR TRADERS (our users, free): business tools they use every day.
Builders run jobs in stages, with photo evidence and a sign-off from
the client. Spaza shops use tools such as a credit book, and order
stock from their suppliers. Akayza shows each trader the suppliers
near them who sell what they buy. Payments made in the app are
confirmed directly by the payment provider, and the trader's records
are tamper-evident, so their history holds up when they show it to a
client, supplier or funder.

FOR SUPPLIERS (our customers): wholesalers, distributors and hardware
stores receive orders and payments from traders straight into their
own systems through our integration API, and strengthen their
relationship with the traders who buy from them.

Hybrid by design: traders are our users, and they use Akayza free to
run their business. Suppliers are our customers: Akayza helps them
serve and keep the traders who already buy from them.


--- TECHNOLOGIES: List all the technologies used --------------------------

- Mobile: React Native + Expo (TypeScript), Expo Router; secrets in
  the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore)
- Backend: Python + Flask, domain-driven design, PostgreSQL with
  versioned migrations, REST API
- Sign-in: email verification, JWT with rotating refresh tokens,
  bcrypt, rate limiting, account lockout; Google Sign-In (in progress)
- Device security: an Ed25519 key per phone, with proof of possession
- Maps and location: Mapbox (address search and map pin)
- Payments: PayFast (sandbox) with server-verified payment
  notifications (in progress)
- Supplier integration API: API keys and signed webhooks (in progress)
- Media: Cloudinary, with malware scanning on every upload (in progress)
- Records: SHA-256 hash chains for tamper-evident history (in progress)
- Hosting and protection: Cloudflare (WAF, DDoS protection, TLS)
  (in progress)
