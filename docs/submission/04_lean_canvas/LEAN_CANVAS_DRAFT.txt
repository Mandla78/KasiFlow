THE PROBLEM STATEMENT   (max 2 paragraphs)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
South Africa's small businesses and informal traders -- spaza shops,
builders and other informal businesses -- run their business on phone
calls, WhatsApp, cash and a notebook. A spaza owner restocks from a
wholesaler and pays the driver in cash; a builder buys materials and is
paid by a client in stages; credit to customers lives in memory. None of
it leaves a record both sides accept, so suppliers can't reconcile cash,
builders and clients argue over what was paid, and the owner can't show
the business is real.

The township economy is worth about R900 billion a year, R180 billion of
it through spaza shops, yet about 80% of township businesses are
unregistered, fewer than 9% can get a bank loan and 57% survive on
personal savings (Standard Bank, 2025). When government opened a R500
million Spaza Shop Support Fund, only R179.6 million had reached shops by
May 2026, and 354 applications failed verification (DSBD, 2026).


SOLUTION & FEATURES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A mobile app for small businesses and informal traders -- spaza shops,
builders and other informal businesses -- to run the business and order
from the suppliers they already work with. Free for the business;
suppliers pay. Built and tested end to end:
- Suppliers recommended with plain reasons (distance, what you buy, how
  you pay); every supplier ranked, none hidden. Not a marketplace: we
  recommend suppliers, never products.
- Order from connected suppliers: server-priced catalogue, stock held,
  delivery to saved addresses or collection, pay digitally (PayFast) or
  cash on delivery (capped), invoices and receipts with a QR check page.
- Business tools: credit book (who owes me, repayments), order book for
  counter orders (works offline), jobs for builders (stages, camera-only
  photos, client sign-off by link), builder network (find and bring
  partners).
- The tools privately tell the supplier engine what the business needs
  (aggregates only, never shared).
- Secure account: email-verified sign-up, a code on a new phone, data
  export and account closure.


UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run your business and order from the suppliers you already trust -- in
one free app. The tools you use every day (credit book, order book, jobs)
make the supplier recommendations and orders fit your business, and
every digital payment is confirmed by the payment provider, so both
sides can trust the order, the invoice and the receipt.


UNFAIR ADVANTAGE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Built from the everyday tools up: the demand the tools see is data no
  marketplace or WhatsApp group has, and it stays private to the
  business.
- Honest by design: only provider-verified digital payments count as
  proof; cash is at most "confirmed by both"; tool records are the
  owner's own records. Nothing is added together to look bigger.
- Recommendations are explainable and rank every supplier (no pay-to-hide).
- Security built in and tested: 1,178 automated backend tests and 155
  app tests, OWASP API Top 10 mapped, card data never touches us.
- The team won LP Dev DataQuest 2026.


CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hybrid: the businesses are our users; suppliers are our paying customers.
Users (free): spaza shops and builders / trades (plumbers, electricians,
painters and others), plus other informal businesses, across all nine
provinces -- township, rural and small-town, Android smartphone, mostly
cash, buying from 2-5 regular suppliers.
Paying customers: wholesalers, cash-and-carries and hardware suppliers
that serve these businesses and want more of their orders, fewer cash
disputes and a view of demand.


KEY METRICS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
6-month pilot (est.): 2 suppliers and the 50-100 businesses they already
serve; 60% of those businesses active weekly; 30% of their orders to the
pilot suppliers placed in the app; 50% of in-app orders paid digitally;
fewer than 1% of orders disputed.
Year 1 (est.): 1,000 active businesses, 15 paying suppliers, about
R55,000 monthly revenue.


DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Supplier-led: each paying supplier onboards the shops and builders it
  already serves (at the counter, on the driver's route, on its
  invoices).
- Business-led: an owner invites the suppliers they already buy from.
- Builder network: builders bring partners onto jobs.
- Android app on Google Play; later, spaza and builder associations and
  trade events.


COST STRUCTURE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Running: pilot about R460/month (app hosting R200, managed PostgreSQL
R250, domain R10; media, email, maps, monitoring on free tiers). Growth
about R4,000-R4,700/month (Cape Town cloud hosting and database, media
plan, CDN/WAF, email, monitoring). Once-off: Google Play R415.
Pilot ask: R300,000 for 6 months (field agent, stipends for pilot
businesses, data bundles, running costs, POPIA legal review and an
external security test, test devices and contingency).


REVENUE STREAMS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Free for small businesses and informal traders, always, for the core
tools.
Suppliers pay: Starter R299/month; Pro R1,499/month; ERP integration
R4,999/month + R15,000 setup (later).
About 1% on digital orders paid through the app (none on cash).
Break-even (est.): 2 suppliers on Starter in the pilot; about 4 on Pro at
growth.
