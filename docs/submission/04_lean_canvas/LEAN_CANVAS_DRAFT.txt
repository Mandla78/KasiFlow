THE PROBLEM STATEMENT  (no more than 2 paragraphs -- as submitted Fri)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
South Africa's informal traders -- spaza shops, builders and other
informal businesses -- run their business on phone calls, WhatsApp
messages, cash and a notebook. A spaza owner restocks from a wholesaler
and pays a driver in cash; a builder buys materials and gets paid by a
client in stages; none of it leaves a record that both sides accept. So
suppliers can't reconcile cash, builders and clients argue over what was
paid, and the trader can't prove the business is real.

The scale is large. The township economy is worth about R900 billion a
year, R180 billion of it through spaza shops, yet about 80% of township
businesses are unregistered, fewer than 9% can get a bank loan and 57%
survive on personal savings (Standard Bank, 2025). Informal work is about
a third of all jobs in South Africa. When government opened a R500
million Spaza Shop Support Fund, only R179.6 million had reached 2,369
shops by May 2026: 354 applications failed verification and only 58% of
applicants held a valid licence (DSBD, 2026). Real traders can't prove
themselves, and fake ones slip in.


SOLUTION & FEATURES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A mobile app for small businesses and informal traders to run their
business and order from the suppliers they already work with. Free for
the businesses; suppliers pay.
- Business tools: a credit book (who owes me, repayments, WhatsApp
  reminders), an order book for counter sales that works with no signal,
  and jobs in stages with photos and the client's sign-off.
- Suppliers recommended with plain reasons (distance, what you buy, how
  you pay) -- every supplier ranked, none hidden; connect, browse, order.
- Order and pay: digital payment through a licensed provider, or cash
  (with limits); invoices and receipts with a QR code anyone can check.
- A partner network so businesses find partners for a job.
- Secure account: verified email, a second check on a new phone,
  notifications for orders and the tools.


KEY METRICS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
6-month pilot (est.): 1-2 suppliers onboarding the businesses they
already serve (about 50 shops and 20 builders); 70% active weekly;
monthly orders per business; share of orders paid digitally (verified
by the payment provider); how often a recommended supplier is connected.
Year 1 (est.): 1,000 active businesses, 15 paying suppliers, about
R55,000 monthly revenue.


UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run your business and restock from suppliers you trust, in one app. The
tools you use every day (credit book, order book, jobs) help you run the
business and quietly improve which suppliers are recommended to you --
while every order says honestly what backs its payment: verified by the
payment provider, or cash confirmed by both sides. Not a marketplace:
your suppliers, your data.


UNFAIR ADVANTAGE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built for trust from the first line of code: the server prices every
order, payments are confirmed only by the provider's verified
notification, invoices can be checked by QR, and every business sees
only its own data (tested on every feature, 1,100+ automated tests).
Recommendations come from how businesses actually buy, not from who pays
for ads. Starts from relationships that already exist -- suppliers bring
the businesses they already serve. Our team won LP Dev DataQuest 2026.


CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hybrid-facing: small businesses and informal traders are our users;
suppliers are our paying customers.
Users (free): spaza shops, builders and other informal businesses across
all nine provinces -- townships, informal settlements, rural villages
and small towns; Android smartphone, mostly cash, buying from 2-5
regular suppliers.
Payers: wholesalers, distributors and building-supply stores that serve
those businesses and want orders, fewer cash disputes and a digital
channel to their customers.


DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Supplier-led: one supplier onboards the businesses it already serves
(about 50 per supplier, est.).
Business-led: a business invites its own supplier.
A field agent at the supplier's counter during the pilot.
Every invoice and receipt QR check page carries us to clients.
Google Play; later, spaza and builder associations.
