  THE PROBLEM STATEMENT
    South Africa's township economy turns over about R900 billion a
    year, R180 billion of it through spaza shops, yet nearly 4 in 5
    township businesses are unregistered and fewer than 9% can get a
    bank loan (Standard Bank, 2025). Trade happens in cash, credit to
    customers lives in notebooks, and orders and payments happen on
    WhatsApp, so nothing a trader does leaves proof. When government
    opened the R500 million Spaza Shop Support Fund, 354 applications
    failed verification and only R179.6 million had been allocated by
    May 2026 (DSBD, 29 May 2026). Real businesses cannot prove they
    are real.

    The same gap costs owners every day. Construction employs about
    1.2 million people, 38% of them informally (ILO, 2024), and
    builders and homeowners argue over changes and payments that were
    only ever agreed by word of mouth. Credit given to spaza customers
    is tracked from memory, and cash handed to a supplier's counter or
    driver has no receipt both sides agree on.
    [add lived experience + validation calls]

  SOLUTION & FEATURES
    Akayza is an Android app for informal businesses: builders and
    trades, spaza shops and other small businesses. Traders use it
    free; suppliers pay.
    - Credit book: who owes me, repayments, WhatsApp receipts.
    - Order again from the suppliers I already use; collect or send my
      runner with a one-time pass; pay in the app or cash on delivery.
    - Suppliers near you: matched by distance and the categories the
      trader buys, each with the reasons it was picked.
    - Builders: jobs in stages, changes agreed with one tap, stages
      signed off by the homeowner with photos.
    - My record: a sealed, tamper-evident history the owner shares by
      link for a set time and can revoke. Reputation with plain
      reasons -- not a credit score.
    - Sign in with email or Google, then a PIN or fingerprint.

  UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION
    Run your business today, get funded tomorrow. Everyday tools (jobs,
    credit book, stock orders from nearby suppliers) build a business
    history the owner holds: digital payments confirmed by the payment
    provider and records that can't be quietly changed, which a shop or
    builder can show a funder, supplier or client -- without a
    marketplace, or giving their data away.

  UNFAIR ADVANTAGE
    Trust is designed in, not added on: verified sign-in, one trusted
    phone per account with its own signing key, payments verified with
    the payment provider, and hash-chained records re-verified on every
    view. Suppliers are matched by distance and what traders actually
    buy, not by who pays for ads. Our team already won LP Dev DataQuest 2026 with a
    tamper-evident decision ledger, and we have a researched
    60-screen design and real build footage from a township house.

  CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
    HYBRID-FACING: traders are our users; suppliers are our customers.
    Users (free): township builders and small contractors (and the
    homeowners they build for); spaza owners in townships (pilot: Tembisa and
    Seshego, Polokwane), Android smartphone, cash-based, buying from
    2-5 regular suppliers.
    Payers: wholesalers and distributors serving those shops
    (subscriptions); development-finance programmes, fund
    administrators and licensed lenders checking records a trader has
    chosen to share (verification fees).

  KEY METRICS
    6-month pilot (est.): 1 wholesaler + 50 shops, 20 builders;
    70% of pilot users active weekly; 60% of cash payments confirmed by
    both sides; 3 funders or programmes checking shared records; zero
    unresolved chain breaks.
    Year 1 (est.): 1,000 active traders, 15 suppliers, ~R55,000 monthly
    revenue.

  DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS
    Trader-led: "Invite my supplier" sends the supplier a WhatsApp or
    email invite from its own customer.
    Supplier-led: one wholesaler onboards the shops it already serves
    (~50 shops, est.).
    A field agent at the wholesaler's counter during the pilot.
    Every shared record link carries Akayza to funders and clients.
    Download from Google Play; later, spaza and builder associations.

  COST STRUCTURE

    PostgreSQL R250, domain R10; Cloudflare, Cloudinary media + malware
    scanning, email, maps and monitoring on free tiers). Growth
    ~R4,000-R4,700/month (AWS Cape Town hosting + database R1,500,
    Cloudinary Plus R1,630, Cloudflare Pro R330, email R330, monitoring

    Pilot ask: R300,000 for 6 months (builder stipends R96,000; field
    agent R60,000; running costs R24,000; pilot data bundles R60,000;
    POPIA legal review R30,000; test devices and contingency R30,000).
    Break-even: pilot 2 suppliers on Starter; growth about 4 on Pro.

  REVENUE STREAMS

    Suppliers: Starter R299/month; Pro R1,499/month; ERP integration
