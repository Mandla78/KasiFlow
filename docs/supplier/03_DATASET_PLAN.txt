===========================================================================
SUPPLIER 03 -- THE DATASET (synthetic, realistic, integration-ready)
===========================================================================

WHY SYNTHETIC

  Real supplier data would need their permission; a fake that looks like
  a real company would be impersonation. So: fictional companies, clearly
  fictional details (example.com emails, fake-but-valid-format numbers
  marked as test), realistic products and South African prices.


THE SUPPLIERS (fictional)

  Mahlangu Wholesale      spaza groceries, drinks, household   digital + cash
                          Tembisa, delivers 15 km
  Ndlovu Hardware         building materials, plumbing,        digital
                          electrical, tools; Ivory Park, 25 km
  Glow Distributors       fragrance, personal care, phone      digital
                          accessories; Soweto, collect + 10 km
  (+ Kasi Cold Drinks     beverages only, digital + cash -- if time)


FILES  (backend/seed/suppliers/<slug>/)

  supplier.json      the profile (below)
  products.csv       the catalogue (below)
  images/            placeholders we generate (logo + 1-7 per product)

  The SAME files are what a supplier's ERP would send: the loader and the
  integration API accept exactly these shapes (05). Mock mode reads a
  small slice; the backend imports all of it.

  Sizes: mock ~20 products per supplier; backend 150-300 per supplier
  (hundreds in total) to prove import, paging, search and speed.

products.csv  (one row per product; UTF-8; header row)   [BUILT 2026-09-25]

  Checked against how SA suppliers' systems describe stock (Sage Pastel
  "Item Code", GS1 barcodes on units and cases, SARS zero-rated foods):

  product_code *   the supplier's item/stock code; unique per supplier.
                   Never shown to traders.
  name *, brand    clean text (no hidden characters, no = + - @ at the
                   start: spreadsheet-formula injection)
  category *       one of our 21 codes AND one of the supplier's own
  unit *           each | pack | case | bag | box | bottle | tin | tray |
                   crate | bundle | roll | sheet | length | cube | pallet |
                   kg | litre | metre
  pack_size *      "24 x 330 ml cans", "50 kg"
  units_per_pack   24 for a case of 24 (default 1): the app can show the
                   price per item to a trader who resells singles
  price_rands *    189.99 (max 2 decimals; stored in cents)
  compare_at_rands the usual price while on special; must be higher
  vat_rate *       standard (15%) | zero (zero-rated basic food: maize
                   meal, samp, rice, brown bread, milk, maas, dried beans,
                   vegetable oil, pilchards, eggs...). Needed per line for
                   tax invoices. White bread, cake flour, sugar: standard.
  vat_included     true (default): the price already includes VAT
  stock *          what's left (0-1,000,000); traders see only in stock /
                   only a few left (10 or fewer) / out of stock
  min_qty, max_qty per order line (defaults 1 and 100; max 1000)
  unit_barcode     GTIN on one item: EAN-13 / EAN-8, check digit verified
  case_barcode     GTIN-14 on the outer case (ITF-14), check digit verified
  weight_kg        for delivery planning (cement, bricks)
  description, active

  * required. Missing a required column, or any unknown column: the whole
  file is refused, nothing changes. A bad row: listed with its row number,
  column and reason on the import job; good rows still load. Same product
  code twice: the first row wins, the second is reported. Max 5 MB / 5,000
  rows. A full catalogue switches off products it no longer lists (never
  deletes them: old orders point at them).

  Seed barcodes use GS1's in-store prefix 2xx, never assigned to a real
  company. Images (image_1..image_7) come with the images step.

  Load:  python seed/generate_dataset.py   then   flask suppliers load
  (development only; the feed can never mark a supplier verified -- only
  our --verified flag does).


IMAGES (no real photos: we generate them)

  through our media pipeline to
    akayza-dev/supplier/{key}/profile/            the logo
    akayza-dev/supplier/{key}/products/{key}/     1-7 per product
  -- which also proves the real upload path end to end.


HOW IT'S MADE

  A script (backend/seed/generate_dataset.py) builds the CSV/JSON from
  realistic product lists per category (SA brands kept generic: "Maize
  meal 10kg", not a trademark as the product's identity), prices with
  sensible ranges, stock levels, and the placeholders. Deterministic
  (seeded), so everyone gets the same data.
