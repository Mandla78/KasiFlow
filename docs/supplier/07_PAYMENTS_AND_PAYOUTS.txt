===========================================================================
SUPPLIER 07 -- PAYMENTS AND PAYOUTS
How a trader pays, and how the money reaches the supplier -- without
Akayza ever holding it.
===========================================================================

1. PAYING IN THE APP (PayFast)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Trader places the order (server-priced; payment_status "unpaid").
  2. App asks our backend for a PayFast payment for that order; the
     backend builds the signed request (merchant id/key, amount from the
     ORDER, m_payment_id = our order reference, return/cancel/notify URLs,
     signature with our passphrase) -- the app never sets the amount.
  3. The app opens PayFast's page (in-app browser). The trader pays.
  4. PayFast calls our notify_url (the ITN). The order becomes PAID only
     after ALL FOUR checks pass:
       a) the signature, recomputed with our passphrase, matches
       b) the call comes from PayFast (source IP / host is PayFast's)
       c) the amount equals the order's amount
       d) we post the data back to PayFast's validate endpoint and it
          answers VALID (server-to-server confirmation)
     Plus: idempotent (the same ITN twice changes nothing), the order
     must be in a payable state, the raw ITN is stored redacted.
  5. The return page in the app only SHOWS the status we got from the
     ITN; returning from PayFast proves nothing by itself.


2. HOW THE MONEY REACHES THE SUPPLIER (the decision)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  OPTION A -- PAYFAST SPLIT PAYMENTS (recommended)
    PayFast Split Payments pays a portion of each payment instantly to a
    SECOND PayFast merchant: a fixed amount, a percentage, or both, with a
    min and max. One receiving merchant per transaction (so one order =
    one supplier, which is already our rule). The supplier needs their own
    PayFast merchant account; we store only their MERCHANT ID (not their
    secrets). Our platform fee is what's not split.
    Why: the money moves supplier-ward inside PayFast, instantly; we never
    hold it or pay out ourselves; the supplier's own PayFast account is the
    record they already trust.

  OPTION B -- pay into the supplier's own merchant account, fee split back
    Needs each supplier's merchant KEY and PASSPHRASE on our servers (more
    secrets to guard) -- rejected unless PayFast requires it.

  OPTION C -- we collect everything and pay suppliers out later
    Rejected: that's holding client money (regulation, risk, trust).

  TO VERIFY with PayFast before launch (10): which account receives first
  and the fee treatment on a split; whether the whole amount minus our
  fee can be split; refunds on split payments; how the receiving merchant
  is linked/approved.


3. CASH (the showcase; plan 09)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Cash on delivery or collection: the order pass (QR) is scanned; both
  sides type the amount without seeing the other's; match -> "confirmed
  by both"; differ -> "amounts don't match" (dispute). Cash never
  touches Akayza.

  CASH POLICY: see 12_POLICIES.txt section 4 (R1,000 per order for every
  trader, 2 cash orders waiting at a time, the supplier accepts first,
  commission invoiced monthly). Digital payment is recommended and the
  default.


4. REFUNDS AND CANCELLATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Cancelling: cash orders until the supplier accepts; digital orders
  never by the trader (unpaid ones lapse after 24 hours). See
  12_POLICIES.txt section 2. Refunds after an in-app payment go through
  PayFast (API or merchant dashboard -- to verify); status "refunded"
  only when PayFast confirms. Rejected-by-supplier after payment ->
  automatic refund request, audited.


5. SETTINGS (backend/.env; sandbox now)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  PAYFAST_MODE=sandbox, PAYFAST_MERCHANT_ID, PAYFAST_MERCHANT_KEY,
  PAYFAST_PASSPHRASE, PUBLIC notify URL (the ngrok address in development)
  Production refuses to start in sandbox mode or without a passphrase.
