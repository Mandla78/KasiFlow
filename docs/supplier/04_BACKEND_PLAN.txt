THE SUPPLIER RECOMMENDATION ENGINE (v1, explainable, no black box)

  Candidates: active suppliers who can serve the trader:
    delivery: distance(trader pin, supplier pin) <= radius, or
    collect: within a reasonable distance (e.g. 20 km).
  Score (0-100), each part with its reason line:
    + category overlap with what the trader buys   (strongest)
    + distance (closer is better; delivers beats collect-only)
    + payment fit (trader prefers cash / in-app)
    + fulfilment fit (trader's delivery/collect choice)
    + "your supplier" (ordered before, delivered, confirmed)
    - minimum order well above the trader's usual spend (caution only)
  Distance: haversine in Python (no PostGIS needed at this size).
  Output = the shape the app's SupplierMatch already expects.


RULES

  - Prices, totals, delivery fees: computed on the server from the
    database at order time; lines store a snapshot.
  - Stock: checked and held on placement (UPDATE ... WHERE stock >= qty,
    in one transaction); released on reject/cancel.
  - Money in cents (bigint); VAT per product flag; rounding rules in one
    place.
  - Order placement is idempotent (Idempotency-Key): a retried "Place
    order" never creates two orders.
