AKAYZA -- ORDERING AND PAYMENT POLICIES (draft, 2026-09-25)
===========================================================================

The rules traders and suppliers agree to when they order through Akayza.
The server enforces every number here; the app only explains them. These
become the plain-language Terms for traders and the Supplier Agreement,
reviewed before launch. Numbers live in ONE place in code:
  app      frontend/mobile/src/features/dashboard/informal-business/orders/lib/cashPolicy.ts
  server   (Phase 3) commerce policy settings, same values


1. CONNECTING WITH A SUPPLIER
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - A trader orders only from suppliers they're connected to. Connecting
    and disconnecting are one tap each, and both ask "are you sure?".
  - Disconnecting empties the cart with that supplier. Past orders,
    invoices and receipts stay in My orders.
  - Suppliers appear only after their system is connected and their
    business is checked; that's what the green seal means.


2. ORDERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The supplier's price when the order is placed is the price. The app
    shows an estimate; the server prices every line again.
  - The supplier's minimum order and delivery fee apply (free delivery
    over their amount, if they offer it).
  - Every order has one reference, AKZ-YYYY-NNNNNN, unique on Akayza.
    Suppliers' own invoice numbers come from their system.
  - CANCELLING (decided 2026-09-25):
      Cash order     the trader can cancel until the supplier accepts it.
                     Once accepted, it can't be cancelled.
      Digital order  can't be cancelled by the trader, like a stock order
                     paid up front. Unpaid, it simply lapses after 24
                     hours. Paid, problems go through a dispute (section 6)
                     and the supplier refunds if they agree.


3. DIGITAL PAYMENT (recommended)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Card or instant EFT on the payment provider's secure page. Akayza
    never sees or stores card details.
  - The order waits for payment; the supplier only sees it once it's paid.
    An unpaid order is cancelled automatically after 24 hours.
  - The money goes to the supplier through the provider's split; our
    commission comes off in that split (07_PAYMENTS_AND_PAYOUTS.txt).
  - No limit per order beyond the provider's own.


4. CASH
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Offered whenever the supplier takes cash, to every trader. It's never
    greyed out: if an order breaks a rule, choosing cash says which one
    and how to fix it.
  - Up to R1,000 per order (or the supplier's own limit if it's lower).
    Why: a cash order costs the trader nothing up front, so a fake or
    abandoned order costs the supplier a wasted trip. R1,000 keeps that
    risk small; bigger orders are paid digitally, up front.
  - At most 2 cash orders waiting at a time (placed, not yet delivered or
    collected). Stops one account flooding suppliers with orders.
  - The supplier's system accepts a cash order before it goes out
    ("Waiting for the supplier"). It can say no.
  - On handover, both sides confirm the amount (the order pass). A
    mismatch opens a dispute.
  - Refused at the door / not collected: recorded against the trader.
    Two in 30 days pauses cash for that trader for 30 days (digital still
    works). [to build with the cash handshake, Phase 5]
  - Commission on cash: there's no payment to split, so confirmed cash
    orders are invoiced to the supplier monthly. In the Supplier
    Agreement.


5. VERIFICATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Traders: ordering (digital and cash within the limits above) doesn't
    need verification. The green seal shows CIPC-checked businesses to
    suppliers. Higher cash limits for verified traders are an option for
    later, not now.
  - Suppliers: always checked before they appear (integration + business
    documents).


6. DISPUTES AND REFUNDS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Wrong, short or damaged delivery: report from the order within 48
    hours; the supplier answers in the app. Everything is kept on the
    order's audit trail.
  - Digital refunds go back the way they were paid, only once the
    provider confirms.
