===========================================================================
SUPPLIER 01 -- THE END-TO-END FLOW
===========================================================================

THE TRADER'S JOURNEY (the app)

  1. SUPPLIERS TAB -- "Near you": suppliers the recommendation engine
     ranks for this trader, each with plain reasons ("Delivers to
     Tembisa", "Sells 4 things you buy", "Accepts cash"), plus "Your
     suppliers" (ordered from before).
  2. SUPPLIER PAGE -- logo, about, area, hours, delivery/collect, how you
     can pay, minimum order; a category filter; the product GRID.
  3. PRODUCT -- photos (1-7), pack size, unit, price, stock hint, [+].
  4. CART -- one cart per supplier; quantities; running total; minimum
     order check; "Continue".
  5. CHECKOUT -- delivery (to the business pin, or another address) or
     collect (supplier's address + hours); payment: in the app (PayFast)
     or cash on delivery/collection (only what this supplier accepts);
     review; "Place order".
  6. PAY -- in-app: PayFast's secure page; the order is PAID only when
     our server has verified PayFast's notification (07). Cash: nothing
     to pay yet; the order pass is issued.
  7. MY ORDERS -- list (active first), each with status; order detail:
     items, totals, timeline (Placed -> Accepted -> On its way ->
     Delivered / Ready to collect -> Collected), the ORDER PASS (QR) for
     cash or collection, the INVOICE/RECEIPT (PDF).
  8. DELIVERY -- cash: both sides type the amount (the handshake,
     plan 09); match -> "Confirmed by both"; differ -> "Amounts don't
     match" (a dispute, never silently fixed).
  9. RECORD -- every paid/confirmed order is an entry on My record.
 10. "WHAT YOU OWE SUPPLIERS" -- from real orders (stock on credit, cash
     due on delivery), not typed by hand.


THE SUPPLIER'S SIDE (their systems + our API; no app)

  a. ONBOARDING (by us, this weekend by script/CLI): supplier profile,
     PayFast merchant ID for payouts, API credentials with scopes.
  b. CATALOGUE: their ERP pushes products, prices and stock (JSON API),
     or uploads a CSV/JSON file (bulk import) -- validated row by row.
  c. ORDERS: we notify them (signed webhook to their system, and email to
     their orders address); they accept / reject / dispatch / mark ready
     through the API (or, for the demo, the backend does it on a timer).
  d. MONEY: in-app payments reach their PayFast account through Split
     Payments (07); cash is confirmed by their driver in the handshake.
  e. DOCUMENTS: the tax invoice is the SUPPLIER's (they are the seller);
     we generate it on their behalf from their details (08).


ORDER STATES (server-owned; the app never sets them)

    placed -> accepted -> (delivery) out_for_delivery -> delivered
                       -> (collect)  ready_for_collection -> collected
    placed -> rejected | cancelled (by the trader before acceptance)
    payment: unpaid -> pending -> paid | failed | refunded
             cash: due -> confirmed_by_both | disputed
    Every change is audited and written to the trader's record.
