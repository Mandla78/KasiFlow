    - https://www.moneyweb.co.za/news/south-africa/four-in-five-township-businesses-unregistered-as-economy-nears-r1trn/

  INFORMAL EMPLOYMENT -- 19.5% of total employment, Q4 2024 (Stats SA).
    - https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=18255
    - https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=19240

  ROGUE BUILDERS -- government guidance: poor workmanship, unfinished
  homes, builders who disappear after payment; homeowners told to
  use registered builders and keep payment records; NHBRC fines up to
  R25,000 per charge, and deregistration published in the Gazette.
  Vuk'uzenzele, June 2026.
    - https://www.vukuzenzele.gov.za/how-protect-your-home-investment-rogue-builders

  SMALL CONTRACTORS -- small and micro enterprises now over 50% of
  construction employment; finance access a top obstacle. Business
  Day, 16 July 2026.
    - https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-07-16-construction-sector-wanes-as-small-contractors-become-biggest-employers/

  CONSTRUCTION WORKFORCE -- construction employs about 1.2 million
  people (7.8% of total employment); 62% work formally and 38%
  informally, "although it is likely that the number of informal
  workers is higher" (data as of Q3 2022). ILO, "A market systems
  analysis of the construction sector in South Africa" (Nov 2024).
  Checked 24 Sep 2026.
    - https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2025-02/MSA%20Report%20South%20Africa.pdf
