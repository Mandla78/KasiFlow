===========================================================================
V1_06 -- PROOF AND REPUTATION
The record a business owns, how much each entry counts, and sharing it
===========================================================================

This is Risuna's layer (TrustLedger inside Akayza). What follows is a
proposal adapted from Risuna's scorecard. Risuna owns the final
weights.


1. PROOF: EVERY ENTRY HAS A CONFIRMATION LEVEL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every Order Book, Loan Book, Stock and Job event is sealed into the
business's own hash chain. Each one carries how it was confirmed:

    LEVEL                     HOW                                  WEIGHT
    ------------------------  -----------------------------------  ------
    SELF-RECORDED             only the business recorded it         0.20
    CONFIRMED BY THE OTHER    the counterparty confirmed in-app      0.60
    PARTY                     later (accepted order, approved
                              stage) but not in person
    CONFIRMED BY BOTH         Cash Handshake: both parties signed    1.00
    (HANDSHAKE)               the same amount within 60 s

    rev 4: the label no longer says "in person". The handshake proves
    agreement at one moment, not that the phones were side by side. A
    QR handshake may weigh less than a radio one (V1_13 Q9).

    In the app these show as three badges: grey / blue / green-tick.
    Words beat numbers: "Confirmed by both" says more than "Tier 3".


2. REPUTATION (1000 points), AND WHAT IT'S FOR
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

It's called REPUTATION, never "credit score". It describes a track
record to someone the business chooses to show it to. No tool in
Akayza is ever locked by it.

    DIMENSION               RULE                                    MAX
    ----------------------  --------------------------------------  ----
    Trading regularity      active weeks with confirmed activity,   200
                            weighted by confirmation level
    Consistency             steady weekly activity (CV < 0.40)      100
    Record longevity        unbroken chain; full marks at 60 days   100
    Confirmed by others     share of money movements confirmed by   200
                            the counterparty (levels 2-3)
    Supplier relationships  distinct verified suppliers, and how    150
                            long each relationship has lasted
    Pays suppliers          stock orders paid on delivery / on      150
    reliably                agreed terms, confirmed by the
                            supplier
    Integrity               no active flags or open disputes        100
                                                                    ----
                                                                    1000

    FORMULAS (rev 4 proposal). Windows are the last 12 weeks. A "money
    movement" is any paid, repaid, collected or delivered event.

      Trading regularity   200 x (sum over weeks of: 1 if >= 3 events
                           that week, times that week's average
                           confirmation weight) / 12
      Consistency          CV of weekly money-in over active weeks:
                           100 at CV <= 0.40, falling linearly to 0 at
                           CV >= 1.0
      Record longevity     100 x min(days of unbroken chain, 60) / 60
      Confirmed by others  200 x share of money movements, BY COUNT, at
                           levels 2-3. Count, not rands, so size can't
                           buy points
      Supplier             75 x min(verified suppliers, 3) / 3
      relationships        + 75 x min(avg relationship weeks, 12) / 12
      Pays suppliers       150 x share of delivered stock orders paid on
      reliably             delivery or on terms, supplier-confirmed;
                           x n/5 while fewer than 5 orders exist
      Integrity            100 minus 50 per active flag or open
                           dispute, floor 0

    TYPE-SPECIFIC ADDITIONS (shown as lines, not extra points, this
    weekend):
      builder   jobs completed and signed off by the client; stage
                photos; verified-client reviews
      reseller  orders delivered; customers who reorder

    Stages: 0-349 GETTING STARTED · 350-599 ESTABLISHED ·
            600-799 TRUSTED · 800+ PROVEN

    An ACTIVE flag or an open dispute pauses stage progression until it
    is resolved. History is never erased.


3. EVERY SCORE COMES WITH REASONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "You've traded 9 weeks in a row."
    "71% of your payments were confirmed by the other person."
    "You've paid Mahlangu Wholesale on delivery 14 times."
    "Next: ask your customers on credit to use Akayza, so their
     repayments get confirmed too."

Design rules from both original briefs, kept:
    - reward consistency and confirmation, never volume. Size is easy to
      fake; regularity with a second signature is not
    - never reward borrowing


4. SHARING
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Proof -> Share -> choose:
        [x] reputation + reasons
        [x] months trading, activity by week
        [x] suppliers (names, with each supplier's consent flag) and
            length of relationship
        [ ] job history with photos (builders)
      duration: 24 hours / 7 days
    -> a link, sent on WhatsApp

    THE CHECKER'S PAGE (no login): the summary, confirmation levels,
    chain status ("intact, verified 2 min ago"), flags in plain words,
    and one sentence a person can act on:
        "Trading 9 weeks. 71% of payments confirmed by the other party.
         Pays 2 suppliers on delivery. Record intact. No open disputes."

    On the owner's phone: "Viewed at 14:32". [Revoke] kills the link
    immediately.

    NEVER SHARED: Loan Book customer names, individual Loan Book
    amounts, anything about another business.


5. SEED DATA FOR THE DEMO (Risuna)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - "Nomsa's Spaza" (spaza): 9 weeks; 2 suppliers; ~70% confirmed;
      14 deliveries paid to Mahlangu Wholesale; reputation 599, ONE
      point under TRUSTED. (rev 4: at ~680, one more confirmed
      delivery moves the score by about a point, and nobody on a
      video call notices. At 599 the live handshake tips her into
      TRUSTED, and the reason line goes from 14 to 15 deliveries.)
      seed_demo.py ASSERTS before every rehearsal: every chain
      verifies, Nomsa is at 599, and one confirmed delivery reaches
      600+. A weight change can't silently kill the moment.
    - "Sipho Builders" (builder): 6 jobs, 4 signed off, stage photos,
      buying from Ndlovu Hardware
    - "Lerato Scents" (reseller): 5 weeks, a distributor, 30 customers
    - "Thabo's Tuck Shop": a planted R2,340 -> R23,400 edit for the
      tamper demo
    - suppliers: Mahlangu Wholesale, Ndlovu Hardware & Building,
      a fragrance distributor, each with a seeded catalogue

    GENERATE THE SEED THROUGH THE SAME SEALING CODE the app uses, never
    by inserting rows. Otherwise the chains won't verify and the demo
    lies.

    tamper_demo.py edits payload_canonical AND amount_cents, as an
    attacker with database access would, and has a --restore flag so
    the demo can be rerun. (rev 4)
