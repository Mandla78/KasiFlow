===========================================================================
V1_04 -- CASH HANDSHAKE
Both phones confirm a cash handover, offline, over Bluetooth
===========================================================================

WHAT IT IS, in one line for the pitch:

    When cash changes hands between two Akayza users, both phones
    state the amount and sign it on the spot, with no signal. If the
    amounts differ, both screens say so before anyone walks away.

WHAT IT IS NOT: proof the notes were real, or proof nobody was pushed.
It's a record of two people agreeing on one amount, at one moment.
That's far better than nothing, which is all cash leaves today.

It builds on Risuna's Trader Passport spec (22 Sept): salted
commitments, Ed25519, the 24-byte advertisement and the clock-skew
rule are Risuna's. It changes the WITNESS from a neighbour to THE
COUNTERPARTY, and the return path from GATT to connectionless.


1. WHERE IT APPEARS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    CONTEXT                          PAYEE (advertises)   PAYER (confirms)
    -------------------------------  -------------------  ----------------
    stock order, cash on delivery    supplier / driver    business
    counter purchase (hardware,      supplier             business
      cash-and-carry)
    builder deposit / stage payment  builder              homeowner
    Loan Book repayment              business             customer (only
                                                          if on Akayza)

Every handshake belongs to a CONTEXT: an order, a job stage or a loan
entry that both parties already share in Akayza. That shared context
is what makes the design below work. A handshake between strangers
isn't possible, and doesn't need to be.


2. WHY CONNECTIONLESS (no GATT)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Research, 22 Sept:
    - react-native-ble-plx (the standard library) CANNOT advertise, so
      phone-to-phone is impossible with it alone.
    - react-native-peripheral is iOS only.
    - munim-bluetooth (v0.8.1, pre-1.0) claims Android advertising,
      scanning and a GATT server.
A GATT connection adds pairing, MTU negotiation and dropped
connections: three extra ways to fail on a video call. So we use
ADVERTISE + SCAN only. Each phone broadcasts one small packet, and
each phone uploads its own signed half later. The server joins them.


3. THE SHARED CONTEXT KEY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

When an order, job or loan entry is created and accepted, the server
generates a random 32-byte CONTEXT KEY and syncs it to both parties'
phones. It never goes over Bluetooth.

It gives us two things:
    - a short REF the payer's phone can recognise, without the order
      number going over the air:
          ref4 = HMAC-SHA256(ctx_key, "ref")[0:4]
    - an instant, offline MATCH CHECK that an eavesdropper can't
      brute-force (section 6):
          match_tag = HMAC-SHA256(ctx_key, commitment12 || amount_cents)[0:8]


4. THE FLOW
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    PAYEE (e.g. the wholesaler's driver)
      1. taps "Collect cash" on order #1042 and enters R2,340
      2. seals the event in their chain: payload includes amount,
         context, time, sequence, salt
      3. commitment = SHA-256(salt || canonical_payload)
      4. advertises HANDSHAKE_SOLICIT for 60 s

    PAYER (e.g. Nomsa)
      5. Nomsa's phone hears the SOLICIT, matches ref4 to order #1042
         locally, and prompts:
            MODE CONFIRM (orders, where the total was already agreed
            in-app):  "Confirm you paid R2,340 for order #1042?"
                      [Confirm] [I paid a different amount]
            MODE STATE (deposits, stage payments, loan repayments):
                      "How much did you just pay Sipho?"  [R ____ ]
                      NO PREFILL, so the amount is stated independently
      6. signs the attestation (section 5) and stores it for upload
      7. advertises HANDSHAKE_ACK for 10 s, carrying the payer's match_tag

    PAYEE again
      8. hears the ACK, computes its own match_tag from its amount,
         and compares:
            equal     -> both screens: "✓ R2,340 confirmed by both"
            not equal -> both screens: "✗ Amounts don't match. Sort it
                         out now." A DISPUTE is recorded on the order.
      All of this happens offline, in about two seconds.

    LATER, ON SYNC (server)
      9. verifies both chains and signatures, and checks:
           - the payer IS the counterparty on that order/job/loan
             (never just any phone nearby)
           - |ts_payer - ts_payee| <= 60 s (CLOCK_SKEW otherwise)
           - amount_payer == amount_payee -> CONFIRMED BY BOTH
             else -> DISPUTE, both amounts kept, both parties notified
           - one attestation per commitment; a second is rejected
           - the transport (radio or QR) is stored with the result

      STATUSES (rev 4):
           PENDING            only one half has arrived
           CONFIRMED_BY_BOTH  both halves verify, amounts equal
           DISPUTE            both halves verify, amounts differ
           SELF_RECORDED      the payer's half never arrived within
                              24 h (proposed; V1_13 Q10)
      A dispute closes only through a new handshake on the same
      context, or the payee cancelling the collection. The
      disagreement stays in both histories.


5. PACKETS AND SIGNATURES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Legacy advertisement, 31 bytes: flags (3) + manufacturer AD header (2)
+ company ID (2, 0xFFFF = Bluetooth SIG test value) + OUR 24 BYTES.
No device name is included.

HANDSHAKE_SOLICIT (type 0x4), 24 bytes, sent by the payee:
    0   2   magic        "KF" (0x4B 0x46)
    2   1   ver|type     0x14
    3   12  commitment   SHA-256(salt || payload)[0:12]
    15  4   ref4         HMAC(ctx_key, "ref")[0:4]
    19  4   ts_payee     Unix seconds, big-endian
    23  1   mode         0x01 CONFIRM, 0x02 STATE

HANDSHAKE_ACK (type 0x5), 23 bytes, sent by the payer:
    0   2   magic        "KF"
    2   1   ver|type     0x15
    3   8   commitment   first 8 bytes of the heard commitment
    11  8   match_tag    HMAC(ctx_key, commitment12 || amount)[0:8]
    19  4   ts_payer

PAYER'S ATTESTATION (uploaded, never broadcast):
    msg = "KF-HANDSHAKE-v1" || commitment12 || ref4 || ts_payee ||
          ts_payer || amount_cents (u64) || decision (1 byte) ||
          transport (1 byte: 0x01 radio, 0x02 QR)
    sig = Ed25519(payer_private_key, msg)

    TRANSPORT is new in rev 4. The QR path proves less, because a code
    can be sent as a screenshot, so the server records which path was
    used and the two may be weighted differently (V1_13 Q9).

CANONICALISATION: FREEZE ON THURSDAY, OR NOTHING VERIFIES.
    - JSON, sorted keys, no whitespace, UTF-8 UNESCAPED. In Python:
          json.dumps(p, sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":"),
                     ensure_ascii=False).encode("utf-8")
      The default (ensure_ascii=True) escapes non-ASCII, so "Cafe" with
      an accent would hash differently in Python and TypeScript.
    - money as INTEGER cents; time as integer Unix seconds; no floats
    - lowercase hex in JSON and the API; the genesis prev_hash is 32
      zero bytes (64 zeros in hex)
    - INSIDE EVERY HASH INPUT, prev_hash and salt are RAW BYTES, not hex
      text. Revision 3 left this open; it's the likeliest silent
      mismatch. (rev 4)
    - commitment = SHA-256(salt_bytes || canonical_payload_bytes)
    - event_hash = SHA-256(prev_hash_bytes || salt_bytes ||
                           canonical_payload_bytes)
    - the event signature is Ed25519 over the 32 raw bytes of event_hash
    - ONE WORKED TEST VECTOR, checked in both the TypeScript and Python
      tests, from hour 2. It must include a non-ASCII string and a
      genesis event.


6. PRIVACY ON THE RADIO
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - The amount is NEVER broadcast. The salted commitment hides it,
      because without the salt a listener can't hash R1..R100,000 to
      find it.
    - The match_tag is keyed with the context key, so a listener with
      the commitment still can't brute-force the amount through the
      tag.
    - ref4 is keyed too, so the order number never goes over the air.
      It stays the same for one order and changes between orders.
    - No names, device names or stable identifiers are broadcast.
    - What a listener learns: "two phones did a handshake here, now".
      That's the honest residual, and we state it.


7. LIMITS (say them before a judge does)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - Two people who agree to lie can both type the same false amount.
      The handshake proves agreement, not truth. Patterns over time
      catch it: a "supplier" who only ever sells to one business, round
      numbers every time, sales far above stock bought (V1_05).
    - Only works when both parties use Akayza. Otherwise the payment
      is SELF-RECORDED, and weighted lower.
    - FOREGROUND ONLY this weekend. Both apps must be open.
    - Some cheap phones can't advertise. Check
      isMultipleAdvertisementSupported(). BOTH roles advertise (the
      payer sends the ACK in step 7), so such a phone uses QR for its
      outgoing packet in either role: as payee it shows the SOLICIT as
      a QR code, as payer it shows the ACK as one. It can still scan
      the other side's radio packet. (rev 4: revision 3 said these
      phones could act as payer over radio. They can't.)


8. IMPLEMENTATION OPTIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A. munim-bluetooth + react-native-nitro-modules, IF Wednesday's spike
     passes: 24 custom bytes, 20/20 received, at 30 cm and at 3 m.

  B. Our own Expo module in Kotlin (~150 lines): BluetoothLeAdvertiser
     (non-connectable, addManufacturerData(0xFFFF, bytes)) and a
     BluetoothLeScanner filtered on 0xFFFF, emitting {bytes, rssi} to
     JS. Written in H2-H8 if A fails.

  C. QR FALLBACK, built regardless: the payee shows the SOLICIT as a
     QR code, the payer scans it and shows the ACK as a QR code, and
     the payee scans that. The same bytes and the same server path, so
     it's our live-demo insurance.

  Android permissions (API 31+): BLUETOOTH_SCAN (neverForLocation),
  BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT. API 30 and below: BLUETOOTH,
  BLUETOOTH_ADMIN, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION. Emulators can't test any of
  this, so two real phones are needed from Wednesday.

  RANDOM BYTES (rev 4): keys and salts come from expo-crypto
  (getRandomBytes). As far as we know, Hermes has no
  crypto.getRandomValues, which noble's own random helpers call. Either
  pass expo-crypto bytes in directly, or add the
  react-native-get-random-values polyfill. The Wednesday spike checks
  this (V1_12).
