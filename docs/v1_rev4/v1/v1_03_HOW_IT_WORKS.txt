===========================================================================
V1_03 -- HOW IT WORKS
The tools, how each business type sees them, and the core flows
===========================================================================


1. ONE SET OF TOOLS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    ORDER BOOK   what customers ordered from me: who, what, how much,
                 paid or not. For a builder, a JOB with stages lives
                 here.
    LOAN BOOK    credit I gave: who took what, what they've paid back,
                 what's still out, and a receipt I can send.
    STOCK        ordering from my suppliers: place, supplier accepts,
                 delivered, paid. Also stock bought for cash at a
                 counter.
    MARKET       the feed: my suppliers' stock, what I usually reorder,
                 suppliers near me, what businesses like mine are
                 buying.
    PROOF        my record, sealed and tamper-evident: what's been
                 confirmed by the other party, and a link I can share.
    CASH         Bluetooth confirmation of a cash handover between two
    HANDSHAKE    Akayza users (V1_04). It isn't a tab. It appears
                 wherever cash changes hands.

    SUPPLIER SIDE: incoming orders, customers, catalogue, cash
                   collected (Cash Handshake), and Proof.


2. BUSINESS TYPES: SAME TOOLS, ARRANGED DIFFERENTLY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Onboarding asks one question: "What kind of business do you run?" The
answer sets the WORDS, the ORDER OF THE TABS and a few TEMPLATES. It
never changes the data model and never locks a tool. Every type can
reach every tool.

    TYPE          HOME SHOWS                  ORDER BOOK IS   TEMPLATES
    ------------  --------------------------  --------------  -------------
    Spaza /       money owed to me today;     "Orders"        reorder list,
    general shop  reorder from my wholesaler;                 credit sale
                  Market
    Builder /     active jobs, next stage     "Jobs"          job with
    tradesperson  payment due; materials                      stages:
                  from my hardware store                      deposit,
                                                              milestones,
                                                              final
    Reseller /    orders waiting to deliver;  "Orders"        WhatsApp-
    side hustle   who still owes me;                          style order,
                  reorder from distributor                    pay-later
    Food vendor   today's takings; stock to   "Sales"         daily tally
                  buy tomorrow

Why this is the point: THE INFORMAL ECONOMY HAS TYPES. A tool shaped for
a spaza is wrong for a builder, and a builder's tool is wrong for a
student. Every type gets one app with its own vocabulary.

Implementation: a single `business_type` on the profile, and a config
map in the app (labels, tab order, templates). About 150 lines. It's
the cheapest high-impact feature in the whole plan.


3. THE CORE FLOWS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  FLOW A -- ORDER STOCK, PAY CASH ON DELIVERY  (spaza <-> wholesaler)
    1. Nomsa reorders from Mahlangu Wholesale (from the Market or the
       reorder list). Order #KF-1042, R2,340.
    2. The wholesaler accepts on their phone, possibly adjusting
       quantities, with a reason from a fixed list.
    3. Delivery. The driver's phone (the supplier account) taps
       "Collect cash R2,340". CASH HANDSHAKE: Nomsa's phone asks "Confirm
       you paid R2,340 for order #1042?" -> Confirm / "I paid a different
       amount". Both phones seal it, offline.
    4. Both sync later. The order is complete, and the payment is
       CONFIRMED BY BOTH.

  FLOW B -- CREDIT SALE AND REPAYMENT  (any business <-> its customer)
    1. Loan Book: "Thandi took bread and milk, R48, pays Friday".
       A receipt goes by SMS/WhatsApp.
    2. Friday, Thandi pays cash:
         - Thandi has Akayza: CASH HANDSHAKE, and both confirm R48.
         - Thandi doesn't (the usual case): Nomsa records it, and it stays
           SELF-RECORDED. Still useful to Nomsa, and honestly weighted
           lower in Nomsa's Proof.

  FLOW C -- A JOB WITH STAGES  (builder <-> homeowner, builder <-> hardware)
    1. Sipho creates a job: "Room extension, Mokoena, R38,000", with
       stages: deposit R5,000 / walls R12,000 / roof R12,000 /
       final R9,000. It's sent to the client, who accepts in Akayza.
    2. Deposit in cash: CASH HANDSHAKE, and here the client TYPES THE
       AMOUNT THEMSELVES (no prefill; V1_04 section 4). Both say R5,000.
       Confirmed.
    3. Materials: at Ndlovu Hardware, the store records the sale to
       Sipho's account (Flow A's counter version) and tags the job.
    4. Each stage: Sipho takes a photo IN THE APP (camera only, never
       the gallery). The client approves on their phone, and the stage
       payment is handshaken.
    5. Finish: the client signs off and leaves a review. Only a client
       with confirmed payments can review, so reviews can't be bought
       from strangers.

  FLOW D -- SHARE PROOF
    Proof -> "Share my record" -> choose what to include and for how
    long -> a link on WhatsApp. The checker sees a one-page summary,
    with no login. The owner sees every view and can revoke it.
    (V1_06)


4. THE MARKET (the feed), KEPT SIMPLE THIS WEEKEND
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Five sections, each a plain query. There is no ranking engine this
weekend, and we say so if asked:

    1. Categories (chips): browse stock by category
    2. Reorder: products from my last orders
    3. My suppliers: the suppliers I already buy from
    4. Near me: suppliers of MY TYPE's stock within reach
       (hardware for builders, wholesalers for spazas)
    5. Sponsored: one card, clearly labelled SPONSORED, never mixed in

Rules: a section with fewer than 3 items isn't shown. Silence is
better than a half-empty rail. Unknown section types are skipped by
the app, never crashed on.


5. OFFLINE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - Order Book, Loan Book and Cash Handshake work fully offline. Each
      event is sealed and signed on the phone and kept in SQLite.
    - Ordering from a supplier needs a connection to SEND, but can be
      drafted offline.
    - Sync runs when NetInfo says the internet is reachable. Every event
      carries its hash as an idempotency key.
    - The Market needs data. Images and stock come from the server, and
      that's fine.


6. NOT THIS WEEKEND
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - payments through the app (cash only; digital payment is roadmap)
    - any lending, advance or credit product
    - iOS (Android only)
    - supplier catalogue editing (seeded catalogues only)
    - ranking beyond simple ordering in the Market
    - background Bluetooth (foreground only)
