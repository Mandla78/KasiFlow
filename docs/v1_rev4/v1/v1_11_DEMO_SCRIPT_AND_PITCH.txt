4. Q&A -- ANSWERS READY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "How is this different from Spazapp, Kasi App, HustleCheck...?"
      They each pick one slice: buying stock for spazas, a POS, or
      reviews. Akayza is arranged per business type, it's useful with
      zero suppliers (Order Book and Loan Book stand alone), it
      connects the suppliers a business ALREADY uses, and the Cash
      Handshake gives cash a record that both sides confirm, offline.

  "Why would suppliers pay?"
      Orders from their existing customers in one list instead of
      forty WhatsApp chats; cash on delivery that reconciles; knowing
      who's gone quiet. We asked [validation supplier], who said
      [quote]. Put the real answer here after the calls.

  "Why business types? Isn't that just labels?"
      The data model is shared on purpose, so it's cheap to support many
      types. But what's on the home screen, what things are called and
      which templates exist is the difference between a tool someone
      uses and one they abandon. A builder doesn't have "orders", they
      have jobs with stages.

  "Can't two people just agree to lie in the handshake?"
      Yes. It proves agreement, not truth. Patterns catch collusion over
      time: a supplier with one customer, identical round amounts,
      sales far above stock bought. It's still a long way better than
      cash with no record at all.

  "What if the customer doesn't have Akayza?"
      The business records it anyway, and it counts as self-recorded:
      still useful to them, and weighted lower. The handshake is for
      handovers between two users, and suppliers are always users.

  "Is this a credit score? Do you lend?"
      No. It's a reputation the business owns and chooses to share. We
      hold no money and grant no credit. A supplier may offer its own
      customers terms, and anything more goes through licensed
      partners.

  "POPIA?"
      Owner-held records, scoped and expiring share links, every view
      logged, Loan Book customers never shared, no location tracking.

  "What did you build this weekend?"
      REUSE.md lists the libraries and where they came from. The git
      history shows everything else committed after Friday 18:00.

  "Why Android only? Battery? Feature phones?"
      Android is what our users carry (say this only if validation
      confirms it). iOS limits background Bluetooth, so it needs its own
      design. Advertising for 60 seconds per handshake costs very
      little battery. A USSD path for recording sales is on the roadmap.
