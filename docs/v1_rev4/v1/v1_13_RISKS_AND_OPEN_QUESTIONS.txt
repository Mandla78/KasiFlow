1. RISKS, WORST FIRST
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  R1  SCOPE. Four business types, two roles, books, ordering, a feed,
      Bluetooth, proof and sharing, built by two people in 42 hours.
      Mitigation: the H12 spine gate; spaza + builder are the only FULL
      types (the rest are labels); the Market is five plain queries; a
      strict cut list (V1_10 section 4). "All out" means the ambition is
      big, not that the cut list is ignored.

  R2  BLUETOOTH DOESN'T WORK PHONE-TO-PHONE WITH OUR STACK
      Mitigation: Wednesday's spike; option B (own Kotlin module); the QR
      handshake is built regardless and uses the same server path.

  R3  THE LIVE DEMO FAILS ON THE CALL
      Mitigation: USB-only topology, a scripted bring-up, three
      rehearsals on a real call, two backup videos, screenshots in the
      slides.

  R4  CANONICALISATION MISMATCH between TypeScript and Python
      Mitigation: one hand-computed test vector in both test suites from
      H2. Integer cents, sorted keys, no whitespace. rev 4:
      ensure_ascii=False in Python; raw bytes for prev_hash and salt
      inside hashes; a non-ASCII string and a genesis event in the
      vector; and never hash from a jsonb column (V1_07 section 6).

  R5  REUSED LIBRARIES ARE QUESTIONED OR DISALLOWED
      Mitigation: ask Wednesday; declare on slide one and in REUSE.md;
      keep libs/ small. If the answer is strict, drop it: the libraries
      save hours, not the entry.

  R6  AKAYZA AND OUR EARLIER PRODUCT BLUR TOGETHER
      This is new with revision 3, and it's the one to take seriously.
      Akayza now has the same KINDS of tools as our earlier product:
      an Order Book, a Loan Book, supplier ordering, a feed and a
      supplier-pays model. Doing similar things is fine. The risks are:
        - code or designs copied across at 3am, which makes part of our
          earlier product joint work with the hackathon entry
        - the IP position document was written for "KasiMarket". It
          should now say exactly what Akayza is, who owns what after
          Sunday, and that the earlier product's roadmap, algorithms and
          documents are NOT contributed
        - a judge or sponsor later treating the two as one product
      Mitigation: V1_07 section 1's rules; REUSE.md; the libraries in
      one commit; Akayza's features built fresh and deliberately
      simpler; the IP document updated and agreed with Risuna BEFORE
      Friday (V1_12 Thursday). Mandla decides how far the similarity
      should go. This file only names the risk.

  R7  THE NAME
      Nearby names exist ("Kasi App", "Kasi Money", "Kas Flow").
      Mitigation: CIPC, trademark and Play Store checks on Thursday,
      with an alternate ready.

  R8  "SUPPLIERS PAY" WITH NO SUPPLIER TO SAY SO
      Mitigation: at least one supplier validation call before Friday,
      and quote them on slide 6. Without that it's an assumption, and we
      should call it one.

  R9  JUDGES HEAR "REPUTATION" AS "CREDIT" -> NCA QUESTIONS
      Mitigation: never say "credit score"; "we don't lend" is on slide 6;
      terms and credit only via licensed partners, on the roadmap.

  R10 THE LAPTOP (Gradle + Postgres + Metro + OBS + the call)
      Mitigation: build early and rarely; close Android Studio; EAS
      cloud build as a fallback; test the full load on Friday afternoon.

  R11 FATIGUE. The sleep shifts are in the plan. Take them.

  R12 OVERCLAIMING THE HANDSHAKE (rev 4)
      A judge who knows radio will say BLE packets can be relayed, and
      a QR code can be sent as a screenshot. If we've claimed "same
      place", we lose the room. Mitigation: we claim agreement within
      60 s, never presence (V1_01 section 7); the transport is recorded;
      Q9 decides whether QR weighs less.
