===========================================================================
PLAN 08 -- SUPPLIERS AND ORDERING
Suppliers come in through the backend; traders browse and order
===========================================================================


1. SUPPLIERS ARE DATA, NOT USERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    3-5 suppliers, loaded by a backend script from files (no sign-up,
    no portal). In production, a supplier's own system would push the
    same data through our API; that's the pitch ("we integrate with
    the systems suppliers already run").

    SUPPLIER FIELDS
      name, logo (image), short description, suburb/area,
      COLLECTION ADDRESS + pin + opening hours (shown at checkout, on the
      order review and on "Ready to collect" whenever the trader
      collects; UX PDF v0.3 page 12),
      accepts: digital | cash | both   (PLAN 05)
      cash_limit (if cash), driver phone (if cash), verified: true

    PRODUCT FIELDS
      the supplier file columns (rebuilt with the loader):
      name, category, price, unit, quantity, sku, brand, pack size,
      description, image_1..image_5 (https link or file name)

    The loader validates against the 22 categories (PLAN 04 s4) and the
    unit list, and reports bad rows instead of failing the whole file.


2. THE SUPPLIERS TAB
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    LIST       one card per supplier: logo, name, area, what it sells,
               a "Pays: PayFast / Cash" hint, "Your supplier" tag if
               the trader has ordered before. Suppliers matching the
               trader's type come first.
    SUPPLIER   header (logo, name, about), a category filter, then the
               products in a 3-column GRID (photo, name, price, unit,
               [+]). Scrolls; no ranking engine.
    PRODUCT    tap for the bigger photo(s), pack size, description.
    CART       items, quantities, total; delivery or collect; payment
               method (only what the supplier accepts; choose if both);
               Place order.

    Order stock (PDF p4) is the quick path: "Order again" from the last
    order with the same supplier.


3. ORDERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    PLACED -> ACCEPTED (by the backend) -> for cash deliveries:
    ON ITS WAY (driver assigned) -> DELIVERED
    Digital orders are PAID when PayFast confirms; cash orders are paid
    when the Cash Handshake confirms (PLAN 09).

    - The server re-prices every order from the database; the phone's
      prices are never trusted.
    - Stock goes down on acceptance; a trader can cancel before it.
    - Every status change is a sealed event on the trader's record.
