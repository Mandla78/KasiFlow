===========================================================================
PLAN 03 -- SIGN-UP
Traders sign up with email; drivers only sign in
===========================================================================

Screens: docs/design/Akayza_UX_Design_v0.2.pdf, pages 3-5.

  TRADER
    1. Welcome          [Get started] · I already have an account
    2. Create account   business name, email, password, confirm password
                        (step 1 of 4)
    3. Verify email     6-digit code from the email; resend; wrong email?
                        (step 2). NOTHING below is reachable until
                        this is done.
    4. Business type    Spaza / shop · Builder / trade · Another hustle
                        (step 3; sets Home and default tools; changeable)
    5. About you        your name, area, cellphone (optional, never a
                        login) (step 4)
    6. Pick your tools  pre-picked for the type; My record always on
    7. Home             with one first-time card
    (The device key is made right after verification, no screen.)

    The delivery address is asked at the first delivery order.

  OR: CONTINUE WITH GOOGLE (on Create account and Sign in)
    Google account picker -> "Name your business" (email already
    verified by Google, no password, no code) -> business type -> about
    you -> tools. If the email already has a password account: enter
    the password once to link Google.

  SIGNING BACK IN
    Google, or email + password · Forgot password -> reset link by
    email (one use, 30 min; same answer whether or not the email
    exists).
    Signing in on a new phone makes a new device key; the old phone's
    key stops working.

  DRIVER (cash showcase)
    Seeded with an email + password; signs in, lands on Today.

  DEMO
    Seeded identities can skip sign-up through the dev menu. The
    business-type screen must work live: "Nomsa picked Spaza, so her
    Home leads with who owes her."
