===========================================================================
PLAN 09 -- ORDER PASS AND CASH HANDSHAKE (QR FIRST)
A signed QR for every order, and the handshake over QR, Bluetooth as
the backup
===========================================================================

NOW (23 Sep, evening): this is the CASH path only, the showcase for
suppliers that accept cash (PLAN 05). Digital orders don't need it.
The screens (PDF p6-p9) have BOTH sides type the amount themselves
("neither of you sees the other's number first"), i.e. V1_04's MODE
STATE for orders too. The exact scan order (pass, then amounts) gets
settled while building; the principles below are what matter.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE DECISION (23 September)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - The PROBLEM stays: cash changes hands with no two-sided record.
      The supplier can't reconcile the driver's cash; the trader
      can't prove they paid.
    - What solves it is TWO PEOPLE INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMING THE SAME
      AMOUNT, OFFLINE, AT THE MOMENT OF HANDOVER. Bluetooth was only
      one way to carry that between phones.
    - QR carries the same thing, works offline, on every phone with a
      camera, with no advertising support, pairing or radio
      permissions. So:

          QR IS THE PRIMARY TRANSPORT.
          BLUETOOTH IS THE BACKUP / STRETCH (cut list item 1).

    - V1_04's protocol does NOT change: same context key, same
      SOLICIT / ACK bytes, same attestation, same server checks, same
      statuses. V1_04 option C (the QR fallback) simply becomes the
      default, and the radio becomes the fallback.
    - NEW: the ORDER PASS (s3), from Mandla's idea: a cryptographically
      signed QR issued for every order.


2. EVERY HANDOVER ENDS WITH EXACTLY ONE PROOF
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    SITUATION                          PROOF                  SCANS
    ---------------------------------  ---------------------  -----
    Order, CASH on delivery/collection CASH HANDSHAKE (s5)    2
                                       -> proves payment AND
                                          handover
    Order, already PAID digitally      ORDER PASS scan (s4)   1
                                       -> proves handover
    Collection at a counter            ORDER PASS scan first  1 (+2
                                       finds the order and      if cash)
                                       checks the collector;
                                       then the handshake if
                                       cash
    Job stage (builder's client)       SIGN-OFF LINK          0
                                       (PLAN 02 s4, PDF p14)
    Loan repayment                     recorded by the trader 0
                                       (the customer has no
                                       app this weekend)

    A cash handshake already proves the trader's phone confirmed that
    order at that moment (it's bound to the order's context key and
    signed by the counterparty), so a cash delivery does NOT also need
    a pass scan. Fewer taps at the door.


3. THE ORDER PASS -- WHAT IT IS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Issued by the SERVER when the supplier ACCEPTS the order (the amount
is final then). Shown in the trader's app on the order: "Show this at
delivery or collection." Stored on the phone, so it works offline.

  THE PASS (signed once, by the server)
      v              1
      pass_id        16 random bytes
      order_id, supplier_id, trader_id
      trader_pub     the trader's registered device key (32 bytes)
      amount_cents   the accepted total
      pay            cash | paid
      fulfil         delivery | collect
      exp            expiry (Unix s): accepted + 14 days
      server_sig     Ed25519 by the SERVER'S PASS KEY over the
                     canonical bytes above (V1_04 s5 rules)

  THE QR SHOWN (changes every 15 seconds)
      pass || t || live_sig
      t         the trader phone's current time (Unix s)
      live_sig  Ed25519 by the TRADER'S device key over
                "KF-PASS-v1" || pass_id || t

  WHY THE LIVE SIGNATURE: a plain signed QR can be screenshotted and
  sent to someone else, who then collects the goods. With the live
  signature, the code is only valid for 60 seconds and only the
  trader's own phone (its Keystore key) can make a new one. A
  screenshot is useless a minute later. (It also closes most of
  V1_13 Q9's worry about QR screenshots.)

  SIZE: ~300 bytes, binary, base64url in the QR. Error correction M.
  Screen brightness to max while the pass is shown.


4. SCANNING THE PASS (the driver's phone, OFFLINE)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The driver app checks, with no signal:
    1. server_sig is valid under the server's PASS PUBLIC KEY (built
       into the app)
    2. supplier_id is MY supplier
    3. exp not passed; pass_id not already used on this phone
    4. live_sig is valid under trader_pub
    5. |t - my clock| <= 60 s   (else "Ask them to refresh the code")
  All good ->  "✓ Order #1042 · Nomsa's Spaza · R2,340 · PAID"
               or "· CASH -> [ Collect cash ]" (s5)

  For a PAID order the driver taps [ Handed over ]:
    the driver's phone seals a HANDOVER event, signed with the DRIVER key,
    that INCLUDES the trader's live_sig and t. So one scan puts BOTH
    signatures in the record: the trader's phone presented the pass
    (live_sig) and the driver handed over (driver sig).
    -> HANDED_OVER, confirmed by both.

  SERVER, ON SYNC: verifies both signatures, that the driver
  belongs to the supplier and was assigned,
  the 60 s window, and that pass_id is used ONCE. A second use ->
  rejected + a PASS_REUSE flag.


5. THE CASH HANDSHAKE OVER QR (V1_04, unchanged, QR transport)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    DRIVER (payee)                         TRADER (payer)
    [ Collect cash ] on order #1042
    enters the cash counted: R2,340
    seals the event, shows SOLICIT QR  --> scans it (scan 1)
                                           ref4 -> order #1042 found
                                           "Confirm you paid R2,340
                                            for order #1042?"
                                           [Confirm] [I paid a
                                            different amount]
                                           signs the attestation
                                           (transport = QR)
    scans the ACK QR (scan 2)          <-- shows ACK QR
    match_tag equal?
      yes -> both: "✓ R2,340 confirmed by both"
      no  -> both: "✗ Amounts don't match. Sort it out now." DISPUTE

  - Bytes, canonicalisation, signatures, the 60 s window and the
    statuses are exactly V1_04 s4-s5.
  - In QR form the ACK MAY also carry the payer's full signed
    attestation (it fits easily). Then the driver's phone uploads BOTH
    halves, so the result reaches the supplier even if the trader's
    phone doesn't sync for days. The amount is on screen only for the
    person scanning, who is the counterparty anyway.
  - The trader's phone shows the result as soon as it has shown the
    ACK and the driver's side confirms; if the trader walks off early,
    their phone shows the final status on its next sync.

  BLUETOOTH (backup): the same SOLICIT / ACK bytes over advertise +
  scan (V1_04 s5, s8). A [ Use Bluetooth ] toggle on the handshake
  screen. Built only after everything on the NEVER CUT list works.


6. KEYS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    SERVER PASS KEY   Ed25519, generated once, private half in the
                      server's .env (never committed), public half in
                      the app's config. Rotation = new app build
                      (fine for the weekend; roadmap: a key id in the
                      pass + a list of valid public keys).
    TRADER / DRIVER   device keys (PLAN 02 s3)
    SERVER SEAL KEY   for backend actions (order accepted, PayFast paid). Separate from the
                      pass key: one key, one job.


7. THE RECORD AND PROOF
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    CONFIRMATION LEVELS (V1_06, extended)
      CONFIRMED_BY_BOTH   cash handshake, amounts equal
      DISPUTE             cash handshake, amounts differ
      PAID_DIGITALLY      PayFast ITN (PLAN 05)
      HANDED_OVER         order pass scanned, both signatures
      SELF_RECORDED       one side only

    PROOF shows, per order: how it was paid, and how it was handed
    over. A shared record shows counts ("41 deliveries handed over and
    confirmed by both"), never addresses or customer names.
