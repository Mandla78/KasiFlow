===========================================================================
PLAN 01 -- APP SCREENS AND TABS
The trader app, page by page against the screens PDF
===========================================================================

Screens: docs/design/Akayza_Simplified_App_Screens.pdf. Hints, not
pixel targets.


1. TABS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Home · Account · Suppliers · More

    The driver (cash showcase) opens straight to their one screen
    instead (PLAN 10). Nobody else sees tabs other than these.


2. THE SCREENS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  HOME (p1 spaza, p11 builder)
    Header: business name, suburb, the one number that matters most
      spaza    "Customers owe you R340 · 4 customers · R48 due today"
      builder  "Waiting on clients R12,000 · Walls stage needs sign-off"
    Two small numbers (spaza: you owe suppliers, cash taken today;
    builder: active jobs, you owe hardware).
    Two big buttons (spaza: Credit sale, Order stock; builder: New job,
    Materials).
    "Your tools" sheet: one card per chosen tool, with its live line
    (Credit book, Delivery on its way + Show order pass, My record...).
    "Add a tool" at the bottom.

  ACCOUNT -- the tools
    Every tool the trader has turned on, plus "Add a tool". Tools are
    mini-apps on shared services (p16): one identity, one confirm, one
    record, one supplier link.
      Credit book      p2-p3   (spaza default)
      Order stock      p4      (spaza + builder default)
      Jobs             p12-p15 (builder default)
      Daily tally      roadmap (food stall)
      My record        p5      (always on)

  CREDIT BOOK (p2) -- tabs "Customers owe me" / "I owe suppliers";
    total, "1 due today"; list with name, what they took, amount, due.
  NEW CREDIT SALE (p3) -- customer, amount, what they took (optional),
    pays back on (Today / Friday / Month-end / Pick date), WhatsApp
    receipt tick, "Customer names stay private to you".

  ORDER STOCK (p4) -- supplier chips, "Order again" list from the last
    order with +/- quantities, total, payment method, Delivery/Collect,
    Place order. The full browse is the Suppliers tab (PLAN 08).

  MY RECORD (p5) -- months trading, suppliers bought from regularly,
    "38 of 41 cash payments confirmed by both sides", legend (confirmed
    by both / recorded by you), Share my record, "Shared now" list with
    views and Stop sharing.

  YOUR DELIVERY (p6) -- steps Ordered / Accepted / On its way /
    Delivered; supplier, driver, amount, method. CASH orders show the
    ORDER PASS + "Driver scanned it -- confirm cash". PAID orders show
    "Paid digitally" and no cash step.

  CONFIRM CASH (p7) -> CONFIRMED BY BOTH (p8) / AMOUNTS DON'T MATCH (p9)
    PLAN 09.

  DRIVER TODAY (p10) -- PLAN 10.

  BUILDER (p11-p15) -- Home; the job with stages (p12); camera-only
    stage photo (p13); the client SIGN-OFF LINK, a web page with no app
    needed (p14); HOUSE RECORDS (p15). PLAN 04 s5.

  SUPPLIERS TAB -- PLAN 08. MORE -- profile, addresses, settings, help,
    log out, dev menu (dev build only).


3. ROUTES (a starting point; expect it to change)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    mobile/app/
      (onboarding)/  welcome, signup, verify-email, business-type, details, tools; signin, forgot-password
      (tabs)/        index (Home), account, suppliers, more
      credit/        index, new
      orders/        [id] (delivery + pass), [id]/confirm-cash
      suppliers/     [id] (profile + product grid), cart
      jobs/          [id], [id]/stage-photo
      record/        index, share
      (driver)/      today, scan, confirm-cash
