FINDING -- a deeply nested JSON body crashes every route (500)
Risuna, Friday 25 September 2026. Found by the credit book's hostile-input
tests. Not in the credit book: in src/__init__.py (Mandla's), so I haven't
changed it.

WHAT
  POST any /api/v1 route with Content-Type: application/json and a body
  nested ~500 deep:  {"a":{"a":{"a": ... 1 ... }}}
  -> RecursionError in _register_input_guard's _has_nul (src/__init__.py
  ~line 160), which walks the parsed JSON recursively. In production that
  is a 500; in tests it propagates. Checked on /auth/login, /auth/register,
  /me/business-profile and all credit book writes: same crash.

WHY IT MATTERS
  Cheap to send, needs no account (login and register are public), and a
  500 on the sign-in route is exactly what a reviewer's fuzzer reports.

OPTIONS
  a) Walk the JSON with an explicit stack (no recursion) and a depth cap:
     deeper than, say, 32 levels -> 400 INVALID_BODY. No real request of
     ours goes past 3 levels.
  b) Catch RecursionError around _has_nul and answer 400.
  I'd go with (a): it answers early and never recurses.

TEST

  ::test_deeply_nested_json_never_crashes is marked xfail(strict=True):
  when the guard is fixed it starts passing, pytest flags it, and the
  mark comes off.
