0. WHAT IT IS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Alerts the SERVER makes when something happens that the trader should
  know about -- above all when SOMEONE ELSE acts: a supplier moves their
  order, a client signs off a stage, another builder invites them or says
  "I paid". Two tabs: Orders | Inbox. Read state lives on the server, so
  the badge is the same on every phone. The app asks every 20 s while it's
  open (no push this weekend).

  Today: shared/notifications can publish (NotificationEvent with ready-made
  title/body strings, tabs STOCK_ORDER/INBOX) but NOTHING persists it, and
  the app's alerts are made on the phone (notificationStore + mock.ts).

  What the dev database says (Risuna's, read-only count, 26 Sep): 27 jobs
  (31 stages client-confirmed, 3 sign-off links open), 5 partner invites
  (1 pending, 4 accepted), 4 open help posts, 10 order-book orders, 5
  credit entries, 9 trusted phones, 17 auth.login_failed -- and 0
  suppliers / 0 supplier orders (`flask suppliers load` not run there).
  So on a fresh dev machine the orders tab stays empty until suppliers are
  loaded, while the tools already have real events to alert about; hence
  the NEW builder-network, job-done, account-locked and due-today alerts.


1. DATA (schema platform; migration 0023_notifications)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  notifications
    id, user_id (-> identity.users, CASCADE), tab ('orders' | 'inbox'),
    kind (a template key, e.g. 'order.accepted'), params (JSONB, filled
    by the server), link_type + link_id (what tapping opens, or null),
    dedupe_key, read_at, created_at (BaseModel)
    UNIQUE (user_id, dedupe_key)          -- the same event lands once
    INDEX  (user_id, tab, created_at DESC) -- the list and the counts
    CHECK  tab IN (...), kind IN (the template keys)
  notification_settings
    user_id (unique), orders (default true), jobs (default true), credit
    (NEW, default true). Security isn't stored: it's always on.
  Rows older than 90 days are deleted by the existing scheduler
  (master_scheduler, next to the identity clean-up).


2. SHARED EVENT (shared/notifications -- Mandla's area, Q2)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NotificationEvent becomes:
    recipient_user_id, template (key), params (dict), dedupe_key,
    link_type / link_id (optional)
  The tab and the topic (orders / jobs / credit / security) come from the
  template, so a caller can't put an order alert in the wrong tab or skip
  a switch. publish_notification() stays: enqueue, never raises. The
  persisting side is a NEW domain, domains/platform/notifications
  (models, repository, service, templates, routes), registered as a
  listener like security/audit. It never imports a publishing feature.

4. TEMPLATES (server-side; the title is fixed text, params only in the body)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  tab orders, topic orders:
    order.sent             Order sent          "{ref} · {total} to {supplier}. Waiting for them to accept."
    order.awaiting_payment Pay to send it      "{ref} · {total}. Pay in the app to send it to {supplier}."
    order.accepted         Order accepted      "{supplier} accepted {ref}."
    order.rejected         Order not accepted  "{supplier} can't take {ref}. Nothing was charged." *
    order.on_its_way       On its way          "{supplier} is delivering {ref}." (+ "Have {total} cash ready." for cash)
    order.ready            Ready to collect    "{ref} is ready at {supplier}."
    order.delivered        Delivered           "{ref} was delivered."
    order.collected        Collected           "You collected {ref}."
    order.paid             Paid in the app     "{total} for {ref}, confirmed by the payment provider."
    order.expired          Order lapsed        "{ref} wasn't paid within 24 h, so it was never sent."
  tab inbox, topic security (always on):
    account.new_phone         New phone signed in   "Your account was opened on a new phone. Not you? Change your password."
    account.password_changed  Password changed      "Your password was changed. Not you? Reset it now."
    account.phones_signed_out Other phones signed out "Every other phone was signed out of your account."
    account.locked (NEW)      Account locked        "Sign-in is paused for {minutes} minutes to keep your account safe. If that wasn't you, change your password." (never says why)
  tab inbox, topic jobs:
    job.stage_confirmed       Stage confirmed       "The client confirmed {stage} of {job}: {amount}."
    job.amounts_dont_match    Amounts don't match   "The amounts for {stage} of {job} differ. Both are kept."
    job.not_yet               Not signed off yet    "{stage} of {job} isn't signed off yet. See the note."
    job.done (NEW)            Job done              "{job}: every stage confirmed by the client."
    partner.invited (NEW)     New job invite        "{owner} invites you: {trade}, {stages} · {pay} · starts {day}."
    partner.accepted (NEW)    Invite accepted       "{partner} is on {job}. You have each other's number now."
    partner.declined (NEW)    Invite declined       "{partner} can't do {job}."
    partner.payment_recorded (NEW) Confirm a payment "{owner} says they paid you {amount}. Confirm what you got."
    partner.payment_confirmed (NEW) Payment confirmed "{partner} confirmed {amount}."
    partner.payment_mismatch (NEW)  Amounts don't match "{partner} says they got a different amount. Both are kept."
    help.interested (NEW)     Someone is interested "{builder} can do your {trade} post."
    help.picked (NEW)         You're on a job       "{owner} picked you: {trade}, {stages} in {suburb} · {pay}."
  tab inbox, topic credit:
    credit.due_today (NEW)    Pay-backs due today   "{customers} due to pay you back today: {amount}." (1 customer / 3 customers)

  * wording to check with Mandla. Params are names and numbers the SERVER
  already has (supplier, ref, total, stage, job title, builder first name,
  pay); never a client's name or phone, never an IP, place, email or
  device name, never anything a user typed into a free-text note. A
  partner alert never shows the client's price (the network's rule). Only
  order.paid says "paid", and only after the provider-verified path.
  (Q7: the builder's OWN job title in their own alerts -- "Room extension
  is done" -- the jobs screens already show it.)


5. ENDPOINTS (/api/v1, @auth_required, per-user limits)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GET  /me/notifications?tab=orders|inbox&before=<id>&limit=20
        -> {notifications: [{id, tab, kind, icon, title, body,
            link: {type, id} | null, created_at, read_at}], next_before}
        newest first; limit 1..50                         120/min
  GET  /me/notifications/unread
        -> {orders: n, inbox: n, latest_id}  (the 20 s poll; also makes
           the day's credit.due_today once)              120/min
  POST /me/notifications/<id>/read -> {notification, unread}   120/min
  POST /me/notifications/read-all {tab} -> {unread}             30/min
  GET  /me/notification-settings -> {orders, jobs, credit, security: true}
  PUT  /me/notification-settings {orders, jobs, credit}          30/min
        ("security": false -> 422)
  Not yours -> 404. A switch that's off stops NEW rows of that topic;
  security is always written.

8. SECURITY BY DESIGN (for the PR)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IDOR     every query WHERE user_id = current_user().id; 404 for others
  Input    strict schemas; tab an enum; limit capped at 50
  Content  templates only, server-filled params; no user-typed text in
           titles; no IP / place / email / device name in security
           alerts; no client name or phone in job alerts; no client
           price in partner alerts
  Abuse    rate limits above; dedupe_key unique; 90-day clean-up
  Audit    notification.settings_changed (which switch, on/off). Reading
           isn't audited (too noisy, not disputable).
  Honest   an order alert is not proof of payment: "paid in the app" only
           after mark_paid (the provider-verified path)
