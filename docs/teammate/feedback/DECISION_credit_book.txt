0. THE BIG ONE -- THE CREDIT BOOK IS CUSTOMERS ONLY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Remove supplier debts completely:
    - the "I owe suppliers" tab and CreditTabs (show the customer list
      directly)
    - the "Add supplier debt" screen and its route
      (app/.../credit-book/supplier-debt.tsx)
    - kind / supplier_debt / supplier_name from types.ts, the mock, the
      contract and the backend. credit_entries always has a customer_id;
      there is no kind column.

  Why: money between a trader and a supplier only exists through an ORDER
  (paid in the app, cash confirmed by both sides at delivery, or stock
  taken on credit). That is the supplier/order mechanism Mandla is
  building. A hand-typed supplier debt would invent a supplier and a debt
  the supplier never confirmed, which breaks "confirmed by both".
  "What you owe suppliers" will come from real orders, on Mandla's side
  (docs/teammate/01 seam c, updated).
