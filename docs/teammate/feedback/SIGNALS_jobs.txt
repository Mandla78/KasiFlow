THE FUNCTION (read-only, in the jobs domain; never a repository)

    from src.domains.informal_trader.jobs.services import jobs_service
    jobs_service.demand_signals(user) ->
      {"categories": {"building_materials": 0.6, "plumbing": 0.4},
       "active_sites": [{"lat": -26.0, "lng": 28.23}],
       "next_stages": ["walls", "roof"]}

  categories    shares (sum 1.0, 2 decimals, biggest first) of the work
                STILL TO DO: every stage not yet confirmed counts with its
                amount. Its name picks the categories ("Bathroom pipes" ->
                plumbing, "Roof" -> building_materials + tools_hardware,
                "Plug points" -> electrical, "Floor tiles" ->
                paint_finishes, "Gate" -> tools_hardware +
                building_materials). A name we don't recognise ("Deposit")
                falls back to the builder's trade (the same trade ->
                categories as the app's TRADES). No open stages: the trade
                alone. Codes are shared/constants/categories.py.
  active_sites  where to deliver: jobs have no pin of their own yet, so
                it's the builder's business pin, rounded to 0.01 (~1 km),
                and only while they have an active job. Empty otherwise.
  next_stages   the next unconfirmed stage of each active job, lower case,
                each once, newest job first, at most 5.

  Done jobs and jobs in the bin don't count.


PRIVACY (tested)
  The builder's own data for the builder's own suggestions. Shares only:
  no client names or phones, no job titles, no amounts, no suburb or
  street. Never send it to a supplier; use it to rank for this builder.
  The app should say it in plain words: "Your jobs help us suggest
  suppliers for what you need."


HOW YOU MIGHT USE IT (your call)
  - Rank suppliers higher when they sell the top categories AND deliver
    to an active site; a reason line like "Your next stage is walls:
    Mahlangu Wholesale has cement and bricks".
  - In a supplier's catalogue, show those categories first.


TESTS
  backend/tests/domains/informal_trader/test_jobs_signals.py (16): stage
  words, weighting by what's left, trade fallback, rounded site, next
  stages, done jobs ignored, no pin, no client data or money, each builder
  only their own.


ORDER BOOK SIGNALS (built: risuna/order-book-api)

    from src.domains.informal_trader.order_book.services import order_book_service
    order_book_service.demand_signals(user) ->
      {"categories": {"meat_frozen": 0.32, "bakery": 0.12, ...},
       "weekly_units": {"chips": 3, "quarter_loaf": 2, ...},
       "since": "2026-09-19"}

  categories    shares (2 decimals, biggest first) of the last 7 days: each
                item's ingredients x units sold (an ingredient in two
                categories splits its units: chips -> meat_frozen +
                fresh_produce), plus packaging_disposable once per order.
                Ingredient -> category: order_book/constants.py
                INGREDIENT_CATEGORIES.
  weekly_units  ingredient -> units sold in the last 7 days, most first.
  since         the first day counted.
  Cancelled orders and other traders' orders never count. Aggregates
  only: no names, orders or amounts (tested in test_order_book_signals.py).
