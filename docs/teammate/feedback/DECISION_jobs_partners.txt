===========================================================================
DECISION -- Jobs v2: partners on a job, not "connect"
===========================================================================

WHAT:      Changes to 15_JOBS_BUILDER_NETWORK_PLAN.txt after reviewing the
           first build on sample data
FROM:      Risuna, Friday 25 September 2026
APPROVED:  Mandla, verbally (25 Sep 2026), and again after Risuna's review
           of the build on sample data (go-ahead relayed by Risuna).


WHY
  The first build looked like "LinkedIn for the informal sector": suggestions,
  Connect, a feed. Builders don't network for its own sake. They need
  another pair of hands on a specific job, and proof that person can
  build. So the network is built around the JOB, and the profile is a
  portfolio of client-confirmed builds.


THE 7 DECISIONS
  1. Partners come from working a job together: an invite the other
     builder accepts, or picking someone on a help post. No "connect".
     "Save" is a private bookmark before that (the other builder isn't told).
  2. Any trade can partner (two bricklayers to finish walls faster, or a
     builder and a plumber for the bathroom). The job's trade decides who
     is shown: that trade, within the partner's travel distance.
  3. A partner sees their stages, their photos and their pay. Never the
     client's name, number or price, and never the owner's margin.
  4. Tabs: My jobs | Builders | Suppliers (My jobs first). "For you" is gone.
  5. The pay is stated before anyone accepts, like a driver sees the fare
     before a trip: a fixed amount or a day rate (never a percentage of the
     stage, which would show the client's price), plus when it's paid
     (when the client confirms the stage / every day / at the end).
     Required on invites and on help posts. The owner sees a private line:
     "Final pays you R9,000. You keep R4,500" (and a warning if they'd lose
     money).
  6. Paying a partner is confirmed by both, like the client's sign-off: the
     owner records "I paid Thabo R4,500", the partner confirms "I got
     R4,500". The same amount confirms it; different amounts are both kept.
     Akayza never holds the money (plan §5 still holds); it keeps the record.
     This also gives the partner a confirmed income record (My record, banks).
  7. For Sunday: Accept / Decline only. "Ask for more" (one counter-offer)
     comes after Sunday.


WHAT CHANGES IN PLAN 15
  §1 screens      My jobs | Builders | Suppliers; the builder profile is a
                  portfolio of builds (cover photo, suburb, date, "5 of 5
                  stages confirmed by the client", "Built with Sipho"), a
                  build opens a slideshow of its stages.
  §3 connecting   replaced by partners (decision 1). Numbers shared between
                  partners only. Block and report unchanged.
  §4 crew         becomes "Bring in a partner" on a job, with the pay
                  (decisions 3, 5, 6).
  §5 help posts   unchanged, plus the pay; picking someone makes them a
                  partner on the job on the posted pay.
  §6 recommend    "mutual connections" becomes "partners in common"
                  ("Built with Sipho, your partner"); ranking for a job
                  filters by the trade and the partner's travel distance.
  §7 privacy      unchanged (Show me off by default, suburb only, no
                  clients, report/block, rate limits, audit without names).
  §8 data/API     job_crew becomes job_partners (job_id, builder_id,
                  stage_ids, trade, starts_on, pay_kind, pay_cents, days,
                  paid_when, status); new partner_payments (owner_cents,
                  partner_cents, status); builder_connections becomes
                  builder_saves. Proposed in full in CONTRACT_jobs_v2.txt.
