0. THE RULE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "Delete" moves an item to the BIN: hidden from the trader only. The row
  stays (BaseModel.soft_delete(): is_deleted + deleted_at); nothing is
  ever hard-deleted. A binned item leaves every list, Home, Account and
  every total. It can be restored for 30 days; after that it is still in
  the database, just no longer shown in the bin (computed from deleted_at,
  no job needed).

  What a delete never removes: payments recorded on a credit entry, and
  stages a client confirmed (with their sign-offs and photos). They stay
  in the trader's record and in any shared record link. The confirm sheet
  says so ("The R20 paid back stays in your record." / "Stages Mokoena
  family confirmed stay in your record and in any record you share.").


1. ENDPOINTS (all per signed-in trader, per-trader rate limits, audited)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  CREDIT BOOK (/api/v1/me/credit-book)
    GET    /history?q=              finished entries: paid + cancelled, not
                                    binned, newest activity first; q = the
                                    customer's name (contains, % _ plain);
                                    max 200        -> {entries: [entry]}
    DELETE /entries/{id}            move to the bin      -> 200 {}
                                    404 if not yours or already binned
    POST   /entries/{id}/restore    back from the bin    -> 200 {entry}
                                    404 if not in the bin's 30-day view
    GET    /bin                     binned in the last 30 days, newest
                                    first                -> {entries: [entry]}
    entry gains "binned_at": null | datetime.
    Everything else (list, get, summary, customers' owes_cents, payments,
    corrections) treats a binned entry as not there: 404 on get/pay/
    correct, excluded from totals.

  JOBS (/api/v1/me/jobs)
    GET    /history?q=              done jobs, not binned, newest first;
                                    q = job title or client name
                                    -> {jobs: [job]}
    DELETE /{id}                    move to the bin      -> 200 {}
    POST   /{id}/restore            -> 200 {job}
    GET    /bin                     -> {jobs: [job]}
    job gains "binned_at". A binned job: not in list/summary; 404 on get,
    photo, sign-off. A sign-off link already sent for one of its stages
    keeps working for the client (their answer still lands in the record),
    unless you'd rather revoke open links on delete -- question 2.

  Rate limits: history/bin 120/min, delete/restore 30/min (per trader).


2. DATA
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  No new tables and no migration: credit_entries and jobs already have
  is_deleted + deleted_at (BaseModel). Add an index on (user_id,
  deleted_at) if the bin query needs it. Payments, corrections, stages
  and sign-offs are never touched by a delete.


3. AUDIT (no names, no phones)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  credit.entry_binned {entry_id}, credit.entry_restored {entry_id}
  job.binned {job_id}, job.restored {job_id}


4. SECURITY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  IDOR: every query by user_id (tests: another trader gets 404 on delete,
  restore, and sees nothing in history/bin). Input: q CleanText 0..60.
  Privacy: history search stays on the trader's own data.


5. WHAT THE APP DOES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  On the mock and the real backend alike: History (search), Delete from
  an entry or job with the confirm sheet, "Moved to the bin. Undo" for
  5 s, Bin with Restore and the 30-day note. The job's confirm sheet adds
  "The sign-off link you sent <client> stops working. If you restore the
  job, send a new one." when a stage is waiting on the client.


6. AS BUILT (risuna/bin-api, after the DECISION)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Endpoints exactly as section 1 (history/bin 120/min, delete/restore
  30/min per trader; history q is CleanText 0..60, unknown query fields
  422). Every entry and job carries "binned_at". No migration.

  Credit book: a binned entry is out of the list, get/pay/correct/cancel
  (404), the customer's owes_cents, and every summary number, including
  "paid back this month". History = paid + cancelled, the latest activity
  first (a payment, a correction or the entry), name search with % and _
  as plain letters, max 200.

  Jobs (your change 2): DELETE turns off every sign-off link the client
  hasn't answered (revoked_at, in the same transaction; links are locked
  first, in the order the client's answer locks them, so an answer and a
  delete at the same moment queue: whichever lands first wins, and an
  answer that landed first stays in the record). A stage that was
  "waiting" on such a link goes back to photo_taken (or not_started):
  nothing waits on a dead link, and Home's "waiting on clients" doesn't
  count it. Restore brings the job back, never the links. The public page
  also refuses any link to a binned job (a second lock on the same door).
  History = done jobs, latest confirmed stage first, title or client
  name search, max 200.

  Builder network: a binned job takes its open help posts and the invites
  nobody has answered with it (404 for other builders, back on restore).
  A partner who ACCEPTED keeps the invite and its payments: their record,
  and they can still confirm cash they got; the owner can't record new
  payments on a binned job.

  Audit: credit.entry_binned / entry_restored {entry_id};
  job.binned {job_id, links_turned_off} / job.restored {job_id}. No
  names, phones or amounts.

  Tests: tests/domains/informal_trader/test_credit_book_bin.py and
  test_jobs_bin.py (36): flows, totals, 30 days, IDOR (another trader
  gets 404 and sees nothing), an open link answers like an expired one
  after the bin and stays dead after restore, hostile queries, audit.


7. QUESTIONS FOR MANDLA (answered in the DECISION below)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. OK that history moves paid-back entries off the credit book's main
     list (it shows only who still owes), and done jobs off the jobs list?
  2. Binning a job with an open sign-off link: keep the link working (my
     proposal: the client's answer still counts) or revoke it?
  3. After 30 days: computed from deleted_at (my proposal), or a
     purged_from_view_at column?

DECISION: YES -- Mandla, Saturday 26 September 2026. Go ahead with the
history + bin backend.
  1. YES: History takes paid-back entries off the credit book's main list
     and done jobs off the jobs list.
  2. CHANGED: binning a job REVOKES its sign-off links the client hasn't
     answered yet (a job binned by mistake must not be confirmed by a
     client). Stages already confirmed, their sign-offs and photos stay.
     Restoring the job does NOT revive old links; the builder sends a new
     one. Test it: an open link answers like an expired one after the bin.
  3. YES: computed from deleted_at, no column and no job.
  Also: this bin is not a POPIA erasure. A person's request to erase their
  data is a separate path (Privacy Policy); nothing to build here.
