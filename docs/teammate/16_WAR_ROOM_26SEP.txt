---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE 45-SECOND INTRO (say it first)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "A mobile app for small businesses and informal traders -- spaza shops,
  builders and other informal businesses -- to run their business and
  order from the suppliers they already work with.
  Business tools -- a credit book, an order book, jobs -- help them run
  the business day to day, and privately feed our supplier
  recommendation engine, which improves their relationship with their
  suppliers. The businesses use it free; suppliers are our paying
  customers, because most small businesses already have suppliers they
  trust. We're not a marketplace.
  It's built and tested end to end: sign-up, suppliers recommended with
  reasons, connect, order, pay digitally or cash, invoices and receipts
  with a QR check -- and the tools."

  Suppliers   integration feed (checked row by row), recommendation engine

4. QUESTIONS THEY MAY ASK -- SHORT ANSWERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Who pays?"            Suppliers. The businesses use it free.
  "Why suppliers?"       Most small businesses already buy from suppliers
                         they trust; we make that relationship work better.
  "Isn't it a marketplace?"  No: businesses order from suppliers they've
                         connected to. We recommend suppliers, never
                         products.
  "How is this different from WhatsApp orders?"  Prices from the
                         supplier's own catalogue, stock checked, payment
                         verified, a proper invoice, and a record of what
                         you owe and are owed.
  "What stops fake orders?"  Only connected suppliers; cash capped at
                         R1,000 and 2 waiting; digital is paid up front.
  "Is the tool data proof?"  No -- it's the business's own record. Only a
                         digital payment confirmed by the provider is
                         proof; every order says which it is.
  "How does the engine use their data?"  Aggregates only (e.g. "open jobs
                         need plumbing"), private to that business, never
                         shared with suppliers or other users. It's
                         rule-based scoring, not a model trained on
                         people's data -- so nobody can probe it to learn
                         whether a person's record was used (the
                         "membership inference" attack from the security
                         session).
  "Security?"            SSDLC submitted: STRIDE, OWASP API Top 10 mapped,
                         data inventory, card data never touches us, IDOR
                         tests on every feature, incident response plan.
  "What's next after the hackathon?"  Pilot with a few suppliers and the
                         businesses they already serve; Cloudflare, split
                         payouts, offline tools.
