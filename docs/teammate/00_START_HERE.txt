       demo; jobs is the most impressive for builders).

       the mock.
    3. Write the API CONTRACT you propose (endpoints, request/response,

       04), security by design (file 05), and switch the mock to real.
       Another PR.

    camera, hashing...) and write what you find in feedback/ (file 10).

THE PRODUCT IN ONE BREATH

    Akayza helps informal businesses (spaza shops first, then builders
    and trades) buy stock from real suppliers and keep an honest,
    two-sided record of money that changes hands. Slogan: "Keep it in
    the kasi." The traders use the app for free; suppliers are the
    customers. Production-grade, secure by design, regulator-aware
    (POPIA), not a demo.

    The design: docs/design/Akayza_Simplified_App_Screens.pdf
      p1 Home, p2-p3 Credit book, p5 My record, p11-p15 builder + jobs,
      p16 tools as mini-apps. The PDF is a HINT, not a pixel spec.

    The app already has: sign-up (4 steps), sign-in with two-factor,
    Security, Business profile (saved on the server), Payment cards
    (test mode), Suppliers "Near you" (mock). The backend already has
    identity, business profile, media (Cloudinary), audit, scheduler.
