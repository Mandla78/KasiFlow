===========================================================================
TEAMMATE 06 -- CREDIT BOOK (PDF p2-p3)
Spaza default tool. NEVER CUT from the demo.
===========================================================================

  The spaza gives goods "on the book" to people they know; today it's a
  paper book or memory. We make it quick to write down, easy to see
  who's due, and polite to remind.


1. SCREENS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  CREDIT BOOK (p2)  /informal-business/credit-book
    Customers who owe the trader (no supplier tab: file 01 seam c)
    Header: total owed to you; "2 due today"; "1 overdue"
    List: name, what they took (short), amount left, due ("Today",
          "Fri", "Month-end", "3 days late" in amber)
    Big button: "New credit sale"
    Tap a row -> ENTRY DETAIL

  NEW CREDIT SALE (p3)  /informal-business/credit-book/new
    Customer: pick an existing one or type a new name (+ optional phone)
    Amount (rand)
    What they took (optional, short)
    Given on: today, or earlier (up to a year back, to copy in the
      paper book)
    Pays back on: Today / Friday / Month-end / Pick a date (may be past)
    [x] Send WhatsApp receipt  (only if a phone is set)
    Note under the form: "Customer names stay private to you."
    Save -> back to the list, new entry on top

  ENTRY DETAIL  /informal-business/credit-book/[id]
    Who, amount, what, date given, due date, status
    History: sale, repayments (each with date and "recorded by you")
    Buttons: "Record repayment" (full or part), "Remind on WhatsApp",
             "Correct this entry" (adds a correction, never edits history)

  CUSTOMER  /informal-business/credit-book/customers/[id]  (nice-to-have)
    All entries for one customer, total owed, "paid on time" count

  (No "I owe suppliers" tab: what the trader owes suppliers comes from
  real orders, Mandla's side -- file 01 seam c.)


2. DATA (proposal; put the final shape in your contract)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  credit_customers  id, user_id (the trader), name (<=60), phone (SA,
                    optional), created_at, deleted_at
  credit_entries    id, user_id, customer_id, amount_cents (>0,
                    <= 10,000,000),
                    description (<=120), given_on, due_on, status: open |
                    paid | written_off, corrects_entry_id (nullable)
  credit_payments   id, entry_id, user_id, amount_cents (>0, <= remaining),
                    paid_on, recorded_at, source: trader
  Outstanding = amount - sum(payments). Status "paid" when it hits 0.
  Nothing is hard-deleted; a mistake is a correcting entry.


3. API (proposal)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  GET    /me/credit-book/entries?status=open
  POST   /me/credit-book/entries             new credit sale
  GET    /me/credit-book/entries/{id}
  POST   /me/credit-book/entries/{id}/payments
  POST   /me/credit-book/entries/{id}/corrections
  GET    /me/credit-book/customers           (search by name prefix)
  POST   /me/credit-book/customers
  DELETE /me/credit-book/customers/{id}      (anonymises; history stays)
  GET    /me/credit-book/summary             totals for Home/Account

  All /me: the signed-in trader only (file 05 s1).


4. WHATSAPP (no API needed)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Receipt / reminder = open https://wa.me/27XXXXXXXXX?text=<encoded>
  with Linking.openURL on the trader's phone. Message examples:
    "Hi Thabo, Nomsa's Spaza: R85.00 on the book for bread and milk,
     due Friday. Thank you!"
    "Hi Thabo, friendly reminder from Nomsa's Spaza: R85.00 was due
     Friday."
  Phone to international format: 082 123 4567 -> 27821234567.
  Never include a link to our app or anything the customer must log in to.
