THE IDEA
  Akayza already has builders. A builder rarely works alone: a general
  builder needs a plumber for the bathroom, an electrician for the wiring,
  a painter at the end -- and today finds them through word of mouth and
  WhatsApp groups, with no way to see their work first.

  Jobs becomes a place where builders FIND each other, SEE each other's
  real work (photos and short videos of finished stages, confirmed by
  clients), CONNECT, and WORK TOGETHER on a job. Talking happens where
  they already talk: WhatsApp and phone calls.

  After suppliers, this is the second feature that makes Akayza more than
  a tool: a network with proof. (Suppliers for a job come later; Mandla
  wires that tab.)

  RULE: clear and simple. Big photos, few words, one obvious action per
  screen. A builder on a site with dusty hands must manage it.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE SCREENS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Jobs gets three tabs at the TOP (a segmented bar under the title):

      [ For you ]   [ Jobs ]   [ Suppliers ]

  FOR YOU  (the new front page of the tool)
    a) BUILDERS YOU MAY KNOW -- a row you swipe LEFT/RIGHT: round photo (or
       initials), name, trade, area, one reason line:
         "Plumber · Tembisa · 3 km"
         "Works with Sipho, who you know"
         "12 stages confirmed by clients"
       Tap -> their profile. "Connect" on the card.
    b) HELP WANTED NEAR YOU -- posts from builders who need someone of
       YOUR trade: "Plumber needed · bathroom · Ivory Park · from Mon,
       2 days". Tap -> "I'm interested".
    c) YOUR PEOPLE -- builders you're connected to, with WhatsApp and Call
       buttons right on the row.
    d) SUPPLIERS FOR YOUR JOBS -- one row, placeholder until Mandla wires it
       ("Coming soon" card, no fake data).

  JOBS  (what exists today, made clearer)
    Your jobs list as now, plus on each job:
      - CREW: the builders working with you on it (faces in a row,
        "+ Add someone").
      - "NEED HELP ON THIS JOB?" -> make a help-wanted post from it (the
        trade, what for, when, how long; the area comes from the job).
    Job detail: stages as today; a stage can be given to a crew member
    ("Bathroom plumbing -- Thabo").

  SUPPLIERS  (Mandla, later)
    Suppliers for this builder's jobs and stages (materials per stage ->
    suppliers that deliver to the site). Leave a placeholder screen.

  BUILDER PROFILE  (someone else's)
    Photo or initials, name, trades, area (suburb only), since when on
    Akayza, proof numbers ("24 stages confirmed by clients", "8 jobs
    done"), and WORK: finished jobs as a SLIDESHOW (swipe photos and short
    videos full-screen, the stage name under each). Buttons: Connect (or
    WhatsApp + Call once connected), and ... -> Report / Block.

  MY BUILDER PROFILE  (your own)
    Trades (several allowed), a one-line "what I do", how far you travel,
    "Show me to other builders" (on/off), and WHICH finished jobs and
    photos/videos appear in your work (you choose; nothing is public by
    default).


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WORK SHOWCASE (photos and short videos, Cloudinary)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Source: the stage photos jobs already take (camera only, hashed by the
    server) -- so the showcase is REAL work with a client's sign-off, not
    photos off the internet. That is the trust story; say it on the
    profile: "Photos from jobs confirmed by clients".
  - Short videos (new): up to 30 seconds and 50 MB, camera only, uploaded
    the same way as photos (shared.media signed upload, the job's own
    folder). Cloudinary's free plan refuses videos over 100 MB; our cap
    keeps uploads fast on mobile data and bandwidth costs low. Show a
    poster frame; play on tap, never autoplay (data costs).
  - The builder picks what's shown (per photo / video). A client's home
    is private: no street address on the showcase, only the suburb; a tip
    when choosing: "Avoid faces and house numbers". Location metadata is
    stripped (check Cloudinary's upload settings; test it).
  - The slideshow loads small versions first (Cloudinary transformations:
    width-limited, auto quality) -- a profile must open on slow data.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CONNECTING BUILDERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - A builder connection is MUTUAL (unlike connecting to a supplier):
    request -> the other accepts or ignores. Pending requests show on
    For you ("Thabo wants to connect").
  - Phone numbers are shown ONLY between connected builders (or between a
    help-wanted poster and someone they picked, section 5). Before that:
    name, trade, area and work only.
  - Block: they disappear for you and can't find you or message you
    through Akayza. Report: a reason list; reports are reviewed by us
    (store them; the review screen is later).
  - Limits: 30 connection requests a day per builder; the same person
    can't be asked again for 30 days after ignoring.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WORKING TOGETHER ON A JOB (crew)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The job owner adds a CONNECTED builder to a job, with a role
    ("Plumbing") and optionally the stages they're doing. The builder
    accepts; the job then shows in their Jobs tab marked "Crew".
  - What crew see: the job title, the suburb, their stages, and photos of
    those stages. NOT: the client's name or phone, the job's total or the
    other stages' money -- unless the owner shares them later (not now).
  - A crew member can take the photo for their own stage; the sign-off
    still goes from the owner to the client (one client relationship).
  - This is also proof for the crew member: stages they did, confirmed by
    the client, count on THEIR profile too ("Worked on 3 jobs with
    Nomsa Builders").


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HELP WANTED POSTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - From a job: trade needed, what for (short text), start date, how many
    days, the job's suburb (never the address). Expires after 7 days or
    when filled.
  - Shown to builders of that trade within their travel distance, on
    For you, best matches first (section 6).
  - "I'm interested" -> the poster sees who responded (with their work and
    proof) and picks -> both get each other's number: WhatsApp / Call.
  - Limits: 5 open posts per builder; 20 "I'm interested" a day.
  - No payment through us for now: builders agree between themselves.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE RECOMMENDATION (explainable, like the supplier engine)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Builders you may know" and help-post matching score each candidate and
  SHOW the top reason. No black box.

    score =
      trade fit            30  complementary trades rank highest (general
                               builder <-> plumber, electrician, painter,
                               tiler, carpenter); the same trade scores
                               lower but still counts (overflow work)
    + closeness          20  distance between home areas / active sites,
                               within the builder's travel distance
    + mutual connections 20  number of builders you both know (how
                               LinkedIn's "people you may know" works:
                               "triadic closure" -- people with many
                               shared connections tend to connect)
    + proof              20  stages confirmed by clients (log-scaled so a
                               few big builders don't take over)
    + activity           10  active in the last 30 days

  Rules:
    - Never show: people you blocked or who blocked you, people who
      turned "Show me" off, yourself, existing connections.
    - Cold start (a new builder with no connections): trade fit +
      closeness only, reason "Plumber · 3 km".
    - Fairness: don't let the same 3 builders fill every row -- after the
      top 3, mix in newer builders (they need their first connection too).
    - Help posts: trade must match; then closeness, proof, activity.
  Keep it in plain Python like the supplier engine (a few thousand
  builders need no machine learning). Later (with real data): weight
  tuning from which suggestions get accepted.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PRIVACY, SAFETY, ETHICS (POPIA)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Visibility is a choice with a clear reason: "Show me to other builders
    so they can find you and see your work." Consent is specific and can
    be withdrawn any time (then the profile disappears at once).
  - Minimum shown: name, trades, suburb, proof numbers, chosen work. Never:
    the exact location, clients' names or numbers, the builder's own
    phone (until connected).
  - Photos/videos of clients' homes: builder-chosen, no address, metadata
    stripped.
  - Abuse: block, report, rate limits, audit every connect/accept/report
    (no names or phones in the audit).
  - All queries scoped to current_user(); tests prove a builder can't see
    another's pending requests, crew-only job details or a blocked
    person.

8. DATA AND API (sketch -- propose the real one in CONTRACT_jobs_v2.txt)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tables (trader schema):
    builder_profiles     user_id, trades[], about, travel_km, visible,
                         visible_changed_at
    builder_connections  requester_id, addressee_id, status
                         (pending/accepted/ignored), responded_at
    builder_blocks       blocker_id, blocked_id
    builder_reports      reporter_id, reported_id, reason, note
    job_crew             job_id, builder_id, role, status (invited/
                         accepted/left), stage_ids[]
    help_posts           job_id, user_id, trade, what, starts_on, days,
                         suburb, lat/lng (rounded), status, expires_at
    help_responses       post_id, builder_id, status (interested/picked)
    showcase_items       user_id, job_id, stage_id, media public_id,
                         kind (photo/video), position, visible

  API (all @auth_required, rate-limited, scoped):
    GET  /me/builder-profile, PATCH /me/builder-profile
    GET  /builders/for-you            (people you may know + help posts
                                       + your people, one call for the tab)
    GET  /builders/<id>               (profile + showcase)
    POST /builders/<id>/connect, POST /me/builder-requests/<id>/accept|ignore
    POST /builders/<id>/block, POST /builders/<id>/report
    POST /me/jobs/<id>/crew, POST /me/crew-invites/<id>/accept
    POST /me/jobs/<id>/help-posts, POST /help-posts/<id>/interested,
    POST /me/help-posts/<id>/pick/<builder_id>
    showcase: sign/finish upload like stage photos; PATCH visibility/order

  Contact (app only, no backend): Linking.openURL(
    "https://wa.me/27XXXXXXXXX?text=" + encodeURIComponent(msg)) and
    "tel:+27XXXXXXXXX". Numbers stored E.164; strip the "+" for wa.me.
    Core React Native; no new native module.

QUESTIONS BUILDERS WILL ASK (answer them in the UI)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Will other builders steal my clients?"      -> clients' names and numbers
                                                  are never shown.
  "Who can see my phone number?"               -> only builders you connect
                                                  with or pick.
  "Can I hide my work?"                        -> you choose every photo;
                                                  hide everything any time.
  "What if someone is fake?"                   -> proof comes from clients'
                                                  sign-offs; report and block.
  "Does it cost money / data?"                 -> free; small photos first,
                                                  videos only on tap.
