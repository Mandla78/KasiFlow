2. WHAT TO BUILD
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  a) In recommendation_service: read the signals of the tools the trader
     has switched ON (jobs for builders, order book for food / spaza),
     through the SERVICES only (never the repositories: the boundary test
     must stay green). If a tool is off, or its call fails, the ranking is
     exactly today's (catch, log without personal data, carry on).

  b) Demand boost to "fit" -- keep it simple and explainable:
       demand = sum over the supplier's categories of the signal share
                (0..1)
       fit    = max(today's fit, 0.5 * today's fit + 0.5 * demand)
     (so demand can lift a supplier, never push one below today's score;
     the total still tops out at 100). Put the 0.5 weights in one named
     constant with a comment saying why.

  c) A reason the trader can read, only when demand added something:
       builder:  "Your open jobs need plumbing"          (top 1-2 categories,
                                                           their labels)
       food:     "You sell a lot that needs bakery and meat & frozen"
     Use the existing _LABELS. Never an amount, a client, a job title or
     an item name in a reason or a log.

  d) Still rank EVERY supplier. Never hide one. Honest reasons stay
     ("outside their delivery area").

  e) Optional if time: active_sites -> "delivers to your site" when a
     supplier's radius covers an open job's site but not the business.
     Skip it if it isn't clean by 22:00.
