---------------------------------------------------------------------------
2c. "YOU OWE SUPPLIERS" = CASH ORDERS ONLY (the rule)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Counts:  payment_method = cash, payment_status = cash_due, and status in
           accepted | out_for_delivery | ready_for_collection |
           delivered | collected
  Never:   digital (in_app) orders -- they're paid up front, nobody owes
           anything; placed-but-not-accepted cash orders (the supplier
           hasn't agreed yet: shown as "1 waiting for the supplier" in
           the note, not in the amount); rejected / cancelled / expired.
  Leaves the total when the cash is settled. Today nothing settles it,
  so this plan adds the smallest honest step:
    POST /me/orders/<id>/cash-paid   the TRADER says "I paid the cash"
      - only their own cash order, status delivered | collected (or
        out_for_delivery for cash on delivery), payment_status cash_due
      - sets trader_paid_at (new column on commerce.orders) and adds an
        OrderEvent ("Trader says cash paid"). payment_status stays
        "cash_due", so the evidence stays "not_confirmed": only the
        trader said it. "Confirmed by both" still needs the supplier
        (the two-phone handshake, later).
      - rate limit 20/min, idempotent, audit ORDER_CASH_MARKED_PAID
    The order screen gets the button "I paid the cash" on cash orders
    that are due; the amount then drops out of "You owe suppliers".
  (Question 1: OK to add this? Migration 0024 -- 0023 is taken by the
  notifications plan, docs/teammate/17; check origin before numbering.)


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
2d. ONE SERVER SUMMARY FOR HOME (fewer calls, one place for the rules)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GET /me/home/summary   @auth_required(dashboard="informal_business"),
                         60/min per user, no query params
    {
      owe_suppliers: {cents, orders, waiting_for_supplier},
      deliveries_today: [{order_id, reference, supplier, status,
                          fulfilment, total_cents, cash_due}]  (max 5)
    }
  Lives in commerce/orders (order_service.home_summary(user)) -- orders'
  own data only. The credit book, jobs and order book summaries stay on
  their own endpoints (each tool loads and fails on its own, as now).
  Rules: user_id scoped in the query; sums in SQL, not in Python loops
  over every order; money in cents; no customer or client names.
  Tests: cash vs digital, each status in/out, placed counted as waiting,
  trader_paid drops out, another trader's orders never counted (IDOR),
  rate limit hit, unknown query params ignored/refused.
