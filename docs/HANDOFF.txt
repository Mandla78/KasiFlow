1. WHAT THE PRODUCT IS (say it this way)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A mobile app for small businesses and informal traders -- spaza shops,
  builders and other informal businesses -- to run their business and to
  order from the suppliers they already work with.

  - Business tools (credit book, order book, jobs, builder network) help
    them run the business, and privately feed our SUPPLIER RECOMMENDATION
    ENGINE, which optimises their relationship with their suppliers.
  - Hybrid-facing: users = small businesses / informal traders (free);
    paying customers = suppliers. Why suppliers: most businesses already
    have suppliers they trust. NOT a marketplace. We recommend suppliers,
    never products.
  - Always name BOTH spaza shops and builders. Never list "street vendors".
  - Tool records are the trader's OWN records, NOT proof. Only a digital
    payment verified by the payment provider is proof. Never write "proof"
    about tool data.

7. THINGS THAT BIT US (save yourself the time)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Long bash heredocs with quotes fail: write a script file instead.
  - Two pytest runs at once deadlock on akayza_test. One at a time.
  - Jest mock factories may only use variables named mock*.
  - fpdf2 + PNG QR came out garbled: QR codes are drawn as vector squares
    from the segno matrix (documents/services/pdf.py).
  - A random ticket in a link can contain "R5": tests that look for money
    strings in an email remove the link first.
  - PayFast signature: fields in the documented order, empty ones skipped,
    PHP urlencode (space "+", "~" "%7E"), passphrase last, MD5; the ITN is
    trusted only after 4 checks (signature, PayFast IP, amount, validate
    POST back). Don't "simplify" it.
  - Consent versions are "0.2-draft" (app legal content, backend
    PRIVACY_POLICY_VERSION / TERMS_VERSION, tests). Bump all three together.
